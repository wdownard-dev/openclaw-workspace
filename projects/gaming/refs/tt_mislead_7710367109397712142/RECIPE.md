## What it is
A GUIDE short: one concrete in-game secret (rare supercharged Mustang at the Port Gellhorn motel, suppressed SMG upstairs). Not a reaction clip, not a streamer moment. Viewer gets something to go do in their own game tonight.

## Structure (from sheets) [E]
1. 0-~12 s: first-person gameplay of the exact car, text hook = the promise ("GET RARE SUPERCHARGED MUSTANG AS WELL LEGENDARY SMG"). Value stated in the first second.
2. ~12-25 s: in-game MAP footage (own screen, blurred top/bottom bars) with the location circle, so the viewer can find it.
3. ~25-70 s: walking route to the spot, third person, objective marker visible, ends upstairs at the SMG.
4. Mid-video: native TikTok "Follow" pill overlay. CTA in caption: "Save this route and follow for more GTA 6 secrets".

## Visual grammar
- Full-screen 9:16 gameplay, no letterbox, no watermark of another creator.
- Word-by-word burned-in captions, center-screen, white bold with dark outline, one word at a time, synced to narration (TTS/VO). Not a text paragraph.
- GTA VI logo sticker appears briefly for searchability.
- Clean footage (real GTA 6, no overlays from source).
- Hashtags: #GTA6 #GTA6Secrets #GTA6Cars #GTA6Weapons. Title front-loads the payoff + emoji.

## Versus what we have been posting
- Ours: mostly creator-moment cuts (tower climb, boats, safe, chases) with a Liam VO line. Funny/spectacle, no "how do I get this" payoff.
- Theirs: actionable secret + map + route. Higher save/share intent ("Save this route"), which is what the search-driven audience (William and friends playing GTA 6) wants.

## Take for the pipeline
- New short type: SECRET/LOCATION GUIDE. Needs: where it is, how to get there, what you get.
- Source options: guide videos (existing guide lane: guide_make.py, GUIDE-RULES.md), our own cuts from creator VODs where they find a secret, or footage we capture.
- Hook = payoff stated as text and VO in second 1. Map beat in the middle. Route beat. Save/follow CTA.
- Word-by-word captions are the main visual difference from our current text-line overlay.
- Check rights/spoiler gates as before; secrets are fine, story/ending are not.

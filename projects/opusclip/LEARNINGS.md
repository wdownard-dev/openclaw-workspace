# Project Red Pill: LEARNINGS (news lane)

Direction (William, 2026-11-06): wrdclipz is a **news channel**. Official-source clips on breaking stories, posted fast. Gaming is a separate channel William creates. The broad niche test (gaming, AI, GTA, creator moments) is finished and closed; its record is below. How the lane runs day to day: `NEWS-PLAYBOOK.md`. Per-post data: `experiment-log.csv`.

## How we learn
1. **Log every post** in `experiment-log.csv`: story, source, how old the source was when we posted, the radar signal that found it, OpusClip score, title, then results. Write the prediction before the result.
2. **Metrics that count.** Views are weak (Shorts counts any start). Use average % viewed, swiped-away rate, impressions, traffic source and subscribers from YouTube Studio. Public view counts are read by the tracker; the rest comes from William's Studio screenshots. Read at 24 h, 72 h, 7 d.
3. **Pool before concluding.** One clip is noise. Label every conclusion with n and say "directional" until n >= 6 comparable clips.
4. **Questions the news lane must answer:** how much does speed (source age at post) matter; which story types travel (disaster, crime briefing, court, weather, other); official briefing versus other formats; clip length and title style; posting hour.
5. **Write the why** for every clip with results (topic heat, speed, hook, length, face, platform).
6. **Safety rules never relax** (list in `NEWS-PLAYBOOK.md`).

- **2026-11-08 19:00 ET weekly review, directional (n=18 wrdclipz news Shorts posted 11/3-11/8; public API views rounded above 1,000; tracker snapshots every 15 min):** (1) **Almost every news Short lands on a floor of about 1,000-1,500 views, whatever the story, source, score or age.** 14 of 18 clips sit at 1,000-1,500 (Learjet 1,320, NTSB 1,200, HCSO statement 1,500, threat call 1,200, restroom 1,400, gate crash 1,300, hallway 1,400, gate/jam 1,500, suspect-left-home 1,200 at 10 h, Krakow/CBS 1,200 at 9.5 h, Tirana 1,200 at 5.7 h, 3,200-fled 1,000 at 3.5 h). Both are short (25 s and 7 s), have a visual shock moment, and were sourced 0.4-4 h old. Three clips got almost nothing (Marine 8, cafeteria 3, 367 deputies 1): cause unknown. (2) **Speed does not predict views within the floor:** source 40+ h old (hallway 1,400, gate/jam 1,500, suspect-left-home 1,200) did as well as sources under 6 h (threat call 1,200, restroom 1,400, Tirana 1,200). It may raise the ceiling (the two top clips are the two freshest) but n=2, so unproven. (3) **Correction to the 11/8 09:00 'saturation' hypothesis:** the Citibank follow-ups did not decay. After the 3 and 1 view clips (11/7 15:47, 19:01), the next two (11/8 08:16, 15:16) recovered to 1,200 and 1,000+ within 10 h. Banked follow-ups add reach at zero credit cost. Proposal: HOT = 2,000+ views at 24 h, or 150+/h for two consecutive snapshots, or 2x channel median. (6) Public views are exact only below 1,000 (rounded above); use Studio for real numbers.

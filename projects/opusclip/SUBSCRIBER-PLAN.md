## Facts that shape everything
- 0 subscribers from 4,642 public views. True view-to-subscriber rate is almost certainly under 0.065%.
- Best Shorts (Learjet 1,315, NTSB 1,241) got almost all views on day 1 and 0 subs: feed drive-bys.
- Reported "96% viewed" is engaged viewers only; minutes/views suggests 30-47% real retention.
- Analytics lag 2-3 days: only 5 of 16 videos measured. Re-pull on 11/10 (Citibank cluster, Island Boys).
- Monetization needs subscribers AND (4,000 long-form hours OR 10M Shorts views/90 d). Shorts watch time never counts toward hours. Verified 11/7 on YouTube's Shorts policy page: from Feb 1, 2027, Shorts ad revenue also needs 10M Shorts views/90 d.
- Reused-content policy applies to the whole channel, even with permission or official sources. Current clips add no original narration or context. Do not apply to YPP until narrated long-form exists.
- Channel page: description is only "Posting clips daily!", no Home tab/trailer, news mixed with gaming/AI clips (researcher's read of the public page; William to confirm).
- Nov 2, 2026: YouTube stopped forcing private for unverified API uploads. Still set privacyStatus explicitly.

## Synthesized order of work
1. Measurement fix: retry traffic-source/subscribed-status queries, re-pull all videos 11/10; William sends Studio screenshots (viewed vs swiped, channel-page views, subscriber source).
2. Channel page: real description, banner tagline, trailer/featured video, sections, Home tab. William's account, 10-20 min.
3. Check Advanced feature access (Studio > Settings > Channel > Feature eligibility); unlocks related-video links, pinned comments, links.
4. Series framing on follow-up stories: "Update n" titles, story playlists, related links.
5. Test A/B: on-screen follow promise in the last 2-3 s (alternate within each story).
6. Long-form narrated recap/briefing (serves subscribers, watch hours, and reused-content defense).
7. Opening context card (days 8-14, judge on swipe-away only).
Decision gate 11/22: if no arm shows 2x with at least 8 subs and pooled rate is still under 0.1%, report that the Shorts-feed path is not converting.

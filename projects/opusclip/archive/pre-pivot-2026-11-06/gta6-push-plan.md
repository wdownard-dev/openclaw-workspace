## 6. Measure and go/no-go
**Log per clip:** lane, source and its age at post, hook, length, slot, OpusClip score, YouTube views at 24 h/72 h/7 d, "viewed vs swiped away", subs, TikTok views and profile visits. **Breakout** = 5,000+ views on YouTube in 72 h. **Lane works** if median 72 h YouTube views ≥500 and ≥2 clips pass 1,000 (the v2 rule). Compare with non-GTA clips in the same week; GTA timing is a confounder. n<10 means "not enough data".
**Accuracy list (all must pass):** (1) every claim traces to the newswire or the creator's own words; (2) wording: "Rockstar says", never "leak" or "confirmed" beyond what it says; "Nov 12" gets "from retailers", Rockstar's word is "should"; (3) times carry a time zone; (4) no price, edition or release-time claim until I or Morpheus read it; (5) creator claims (e.g. G2's real-town hook) are labelled as the creator's claim; (6) no story spoilers, no Rockstar footage or music; (7) caption includes source name and date; (8) fix or delete within an hour of a correction.
**Captions and watermark:** OpusClip captions on, hook text first, credit line "Source: @handle". William's plan adds **no OpusClip watermark** (`enableWatermark: false` [own]). **My advice on a WRDClipz watermark:** none during the push. A mid-push change confounds the data; apply to every clip from the same day if William wants it, and revisit after Nov 22.
**Review:** Rule stays: ≥9.7 may post without review, but **GTA clips that state facts get a human accuracy pass regardless of score**. For speed on launch days I recommend a fast sign-off at ≥9.0 (William to approve, as it differs from the current rule).

## 6. Is >= 90 too loose?
| Threshold | Clips (of 8 projects) | vs 14 slots per platform |
|---|---|---|
| >= 90 | 34 (3 to 8 per project, about 15 to 20% of clips) | 2.4x |
| >= 95 | 15 | 1.1x |
| >= 97 | 10 | 0.7x |

Yes, as a quality gate: 90 admits raw 26/40 (65%), and each source needs a different raw number to reach it. No, as a volume problem: even at 90, supply (about 20 candidates per week at ~70 credits) exceeds 14 slots, so posting frequency is set by the 2/day cap, not by the score. **Recommendation (William's call):** keep >= 90 as the candidate gate but treat posting as a ranked pick of the top 12 by the human checks, with >= 97 clips (10) first.

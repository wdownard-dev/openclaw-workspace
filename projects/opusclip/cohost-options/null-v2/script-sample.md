# NULL//SMIRK script style: micro-sample

Rules of the voice: dry, deadpan, one raised eyebrow. The joke is always about the situation, the format, or NULL itself, never about a real person. No game facts unless verified. Short lines (under ~12 words) so the mouth sync and captions stay readable.

## Generic 3-part outro (works on any highlight)

| Part | Line | Expression cue |
|---|---|---|
| TAKEAWAY | "Takeaway: the payoff goes first. The intro can wait in the lobby." | neutral smug -> raised brow on "lobby" |
| MY TAKE | "My take? Four screams and a countdown is a plan. A loud plan, but a plan." | squint -> eye-roll -> smirk-laugh |
| YOUR TURN | "Your turn: should I be smug, savage, or politely unimpressed next time?" | raised brow -> side-glance toward the smirk |

## Interjection patterns (one per clip moment)

- Big number said out loud: "Eight. Announced like a world record." (squint, then side-glance)
- Repeated shouting: "Four oh-my-gods. I'm keeping score." (eye-roll)
- Payoff lands: "Fine. That one earned a smirk." (smirk-laugh)
- Slow moment: "Nothing happening. I respect the suspense." (raised brow)

## What to avoid
- Insults aimed at the creator, the players, or the audience.
- Claims about what the game, ranking, or event "really" is.
- More than one joke per line.

Operators, Operator Skins, and Weapon Blueprints, as well as Calling Cards, Emblems, Operator Finishing Moves, Emotes, Sprays, Parachutes, and Contrails from 
Modern Warfare II, Modern Warfare III, Black Ops 6, and Black Ops 7
 will be available in 
Call of Duty: Warzone
 starting in Season 1. With the new Operator Skin Toggle, you will have more control over the Operators Skins you see in 
Call of Duty: Warzone
 only. 
Check out our article on the new Operator Skin Toggle
 feature for more info.
Modern Warfare 4 
weapons provide the full weapon experience, while all weapons from 
Modern Warfare II
 through 
Black Ops 7
 will continue to be accessible in the Legacy tab of the Loadout menu. 
Legacy Weapons are still usable, but cannot be edited in Gunsmith and do not support the following 
Modern Warfare 4 
features:
Mastery Camos
Reticle Challenges
Weapon Customization
Cosmetic Gunsmithing
Build Sharing & Build Codes
Legacy Weapons won’t be left as base Weapons without Attachments. Instead, a five-attachment version of each Legacy base Weapon will be available, as well as any Blueprint for a Legacy Weapon with their existing Attachment setups. These Legacy Blueprints will all initially be locked and available to unlock through Player XP in the Armory.
New Battle Royale Map: Hajin
For the first time, 
Call of Duty: Warzone
 will feature action along the Korean Peninsula as players deploy to Hajin, a new largescale map that connects Battle Royale to the 
Modern Warfare 4
 narrative.

While DMZ players will feel right at home in Hajin, there are some notable differences between the DMZ version of the map and the Battle Royale version. This includes how the encroaching Edge Circle and Gas moves throughout a match, a lack of radioactive cloud at the Hanuel Nuclear Reactor, the available Vehicles, and the distribution of interactable objects, as well as loot caches across the map.

Battle By the Border
In Hajin, Operators will battle in both North and South Korea across 13 major POIs. Navigate crowded city streets, abandoned buildings, open farmland, winding rivers, and forested hills, creating different combat opportunities depending on where you land. 
Hajin City’s central districts feature towering buildings, barricaded roads, and heavily fortified locations, rewarding squads that coordinate their movements and secure advantageous positions. Rural areas such as Imjin Farmland offer more exposed terrain, where long-range engagements and careful use of available cover are essential for survival.
Over the border, the setting shifts as Operators approach North Korean locations such as the 14
th
Political Prison, Chang-san Air Base, and Deadtown.
Weather Scenarios
Expect to run into variable weather patterns in your deployments to Hajin, adding a layer of unpredictability to each match. From clear skies to fog, wind, rain, and distant storms, localized weather patterns change the look and feel of the environment. With this in mind, the development teams have put in a lot of effort to ensure that no matter what weather you encounter, visibility remains a central factor in gameplay. 
Hajin Gulag
A new Battle Royale map means a new Gulag. When you first get eliminated in Battle Royale, you’ll have the chance to earn your redeployment back into the match... so long as you can defeat the enemy in a 1v1 matchup. 
Eliminated Operators will be sent to one of two versions of the Hajin Gulag. Enter as a spectator including ground level views for the first time, and when it’s your turn to fight for your life, deploy to the central arena. 
The two variants feature a different set of cover objects within a small, rectangular space in a symmetrical layout. In one, partial walls line the perimeter over open ground while the other includes sturdier features with a number of small sheds over a flagstone walkway. Both versions are exposed to the elements and will be affected by local weather patterns, adding variety to your visits here.
You can mantle on a good deal of the obstacles, encouraging aggressive movement throughout the area. Win the duel to deploy back into the match. Lose and you’re out.
New Resurgence Map: Zodiac
Experience Resurgence on the all-new Zodiac, a compact combat zone built around the DRC Advanced Systems drone assembly and manufacturing headquarters in South Korea. Zodiac combines sprawling factory interiors, research facilities, elevated rooftops, underground tunnels, and coastal terrain to create a diverse combat environment.
The map’s ten distinct Points of Interest each offer different opportunities for combat and squad rotations. Key locations include Admin, the central administrative tower; Trackline, where trains travel across a double railway that cuts across the facility; and Furnace, an industrial area built around heavy manufacturing equipment. Other locations, including Solar, Old Mines, Dispatch, Depot, Sentinel, Cooling, and R&D, provide additional options for looting, ambushes, and establishing defensive positions.
Underground Routes, Environmental Hazards
Experience Resurgence on 
Zodiac
, an all-new battleground set within the DRC Advanced Systems drone manufacturing headquarters in South Korea. More than just an industrial complex, Zodiac is an environment shaped by conflict, where damaged facilities, sprawling factories, research labs, and underground passages create a battleground filled with stories to uncover and opportunities for combat.
Across ten distinct Points of Interest, every location brings a different way to fight. Battle through Admin's towering offices, navigate the heavy machinery of Furnace, or explore the research facilities of R&D. Other locations, including Solar, Old Mines, Dispatch, Depot, Sentinel, and Cooling, offer opportunities for looting, flanking, and outmaneuvering enemy squads. From tight interiors to elevated rooftops, the map's interconnected environments keep players adapting their strategies.
Underground Routes, Moving Trains, and Environmental Hazards
At the heart of Zodiac, Trackline's double railway system and moving trains add an unpredictable layer to the action. Underground tunnels let squads slip beneath the battlefield and emerge from unexpected angles, while interactive elements like moving solar panels, furnace conveyor belts, and explosive fuel barrels create new ways to turn the environment to your advantage.
Developed by Beenox in close collaboration with Infinity Ward, Zodiac builds on the team's experience creating iconic 
Call of Duty: Warzone
 locations such as Rebirth Island and Vondel. Combining layered level design, dynamic environments, and a distinct South Korean setting, Zodiac delivers a fresh Resurgence experience where every match offers new opportunities to move, engage, and outsmart opponents.
Returning Battle Royale Map: Verdansk (In-Season)
Verdansk returns later in Season 1, its familiar skyline now punctuated by the Signal Station, the Grazna-7 Launch Complex, Overlook skyscraper, and the high-tech Drone Labs. From frozen forests and industrial sprawl to dense city blocks, Verdansk brings newer landmarks and new reasons to revisit this classic 
Call of Duty: Warzone
 map.
Returning Battle Royale Map: Rebirth Island (In-Season)
Rebirth Island also returns in Season 1, bringing its close quarters fights back across the prison, control center, and surrounding industrial compounds. With repurposed infrastructure and familiar routes, navigable waterways, and hidden passages, offer there’s always a place to drop rival Operators across this compact and classic Resurgence map.
New Gameplay Features (Launch)
Which new gameplay features are coming to Call of Duty: Warzone in Season 1?
New Field Upgrade: Zipline Launcher
Overcome large obstacles and flank from rooftop to rooftop with ease using the Zipline Launcher. Aim and fire the device to create anchors and a zipline that can be used to scale high points and zipline across the terrain. Once deployed, the Zipline Launcher will remain active on the map until it’s either retrieved or destroyed. 
The entire squad can use it, including enemy Operators, so be aware of its placement and whether you’ve got enemies on your tail. If you notice a zipline being created by another squad near your position, check the line for incoming enemies. While traversing the zipline, players can use their Pistol, so be prepared.
New Field Upgrade: Grabber Drone
Deploy a rare drone equipped with a grabber capable of picking up everything from loot to environmental objects and even other Operators. Pick up a downed teammate and bring them in closer for the revive, retrieve difficult to reach weapons and other useful items, and even retrieve Buy Station loot. With a limited battery life, you’ve got to choose your actions carefully, but when it comes to grasping the unreachable, this is the tool to carry. 
New Interactive Objects
New interactive objects increase the level of player agency across the environment, including Fuel Barrels that can be moved around by players and carried by the Grabber Drone. Set them up where they’ll cause the most damage and detonate them when the enemy gets near.
Other interactive objects include a variety of items such as Dumpsters, Concrete Cylinders, and more that can be pushed and pulled, from cover items to platforms and even pyramids that can be placed on the road to stop vehicles from passing through critical pathways. Work with your squad to make the most out of these interactive objects, setting up new cover positions, roadblocks, explosive traps, and more. You’ll find most of these new objects scattered across Hajin, Zodiac, and Verdansk.
Grind for Royalty
Push your boundaries with the new Grind for Royalty system, where your personal player stats are taken into account to generate individual challenges. Royalty challenges reset every season, giving you the chance to try and outdo your prior performances with each new season. Strive for new personal records and get rewarded with two new Weapon Camos (one static, one animated) each season. In Season 1, players can earn the “Gold Excellence” and “Gold Mosaic” Weapon Camos.
Weapon Augments
After multiple Drones fly across the map, and drop portable Buy Stations, you can hunt for these “Fly Buy” Buy Stations to access Weapon Augments, a new way to upgrade your weapon with significant bonuses for that match. These will be available in limited quantities, so don’t overlook a chance to grab one. Season 1 introduces three Weapon Augments, as follows:
CQC Augment: 
Faster ADS speed and sprint to fire speed, reduced hipfire recoil, faster ADS movement speed.
Marksman Augment: 
Improved bullet velocity and damage range, reduced horizontal recoil, reduced aiming idle sway.
Handling Augment:
 Faster reload speed, faster rechamber, faster slide to fire speed, reduced first shot recoil, reduced weapon bob, reduced flinch.
Ground Ops
While moving through the map, look for discoverable Ground Ops that offer the chance to gain valuable loot and other advantages, including high value safes that can be drilled to secure the items inside, surveillance towers that provide critical intel on the enemy position and legendary loot chests, drones that can be shot down for the cargo they carry, and loot-filled radioactive barrels that are located with the assistance of a Geiger counter.
Weapon Trade Station (Resurgence Only)
Upgrade your currently equipped weapon with more powerful version by visiting the useful Weapon Trade Station in your Resurgence matches. Interact with the Weapon Trade Station to exchange your weapon with one of an improved rarity, offering additional Weapon Attachments.
Drop Kit (Resurgence Only)
Boost your next redeployment by securing a Drop Kit, which will provide your Operator with added bonuses the next time you redeploy into the match. There are four types of Drop Kits:
Insured: 
Redeploy faster with all your cash.
Loadout: 
Redeploy with your current Loadout.
Armored: 
Redeploy with full armor, bonus Perks, and a Self-Revive.
Radar: 
Redeploy with an active Radar Ping and supply sweep.
Call of Duty: Warzone: Season 1 Modes
Battle Royale and Resurgence remain the primary game modes, with Battle Royale and Battle Royale Casual taking place on Hajin and Verdansk, and Resurgence and Resurgence Casual taking place on Zodiac and Rebirth Island.
Season 1 launch also introduces three limited-time modes (LTMs), including the Rally Point LTM that is perfect for new and returning players to get their feet wet, plus Massive Resurgence, Loaded Resurgence, and more in the Mid-Season.  
Rally Point
Zodiac, Max Players: 20
A training ground experience that lets you explore the gameplay mechanics of Call of Duty: Warzone.
Rally Point aims to be an experience that allows players to experiment with 
Call of Duty: Warzone’s 
mechanics and systems, while also providing unique elements to play around with.
Discover: 
Explore the map, uncover what’s out there. Find new ways to play each run. Move around Zodiac, complete contracts, take out Hostile Soldiers and try to beat your own elimination high score. Rally Point is a no-circle, infinite Resurgence, and Drop-In/Drop-Out mode, allowing players to come and go as they please. It’s the perfect place to spend a few minutes, or even hours.
Learn: 
Master different weapons, killstreaks, vehicles, and contracts with nothing to lose. Unique events are also waiting in Rally Point such as Hostile Choppers, JLTV Deliveries and Elite Hostile Reinforcements. Rumor has it that a Nuke might even be available for purchase for the most dedicated squads that wish to challenge themselves with high kill runs. Death isn’t the end, it’s how you learn.
Connect: 
Neutral Grounds are available in Rally Point, allowing you to take a breather or form new alliances of up to 4 players with other players. This allows you to find new players on the spot without ever leaving the match. Who knows what might happen? You might find your new Warzone squad that way. Every encounter is a new opportunity.
Earn Rewards:
 Rally Point features an exclusive weapon blueprint and two weapon camos to unlock. Work with your squad to get your hands on these rewards.
Massive Resurgence
Hajin, Max Players: 120
Loot and fight for survival on a large map. Redeploy allies, reposition as the gas closes, and outlast every rival squad.
Resurgence: 
Survive during the Resurgence Countdown to return allies to the warzone. Earn points to shorten.
Wipe Out: 
Keep an eye on your map. Enemy squad will be marked after eliminating one of them.
Survive: 
Be the last team alive to win. Eliminate opponents and escape the gas. This is an excellent mode to drop into and learn the map.
Loaded Resurgence
 Zodiac / Rebirth Island, Max Players: 44
Deploy with your chosen Loadout, earn Perks through eliminations, and build toward Specialist. Keep fighting until your squad is the last one standing.
Load in Ready: 
Infil with your chosen Loadout and keep your equipped weapons after death. No loadout drop needed.
Earn Perks: 
Contribute to eliminations to earn new Perks. Keep progressing toward Specialist.
Stay in the Fight: 
Survive the countdown to redeploy allies. Help your squad outlast the others.
More in the Mid-Season
Stay tuned to the Call of Duty Blog for intel on two more LTMs arriving in the Mid-Season: Rockets and Snipers and Extreme Resurgence.
Call of Duty: Warzone Perks
Equip passive abilities that shape your Operator’s capabilities and give you an edge in the fight. There is a total of 18 Perks available to Operators. The first 15 are selectable in your Loadout, before a Match, and fit into one of three different Slots. The remaining three are Elite Perks you acquire during a Match.
Perk 1 Category (5)
Field Medic
Quickly revive allies to full health, and you gain the same benefits. Increased health while downed.
Hunter
Track enemy footprints, sense nearby enemies, and become alert when aimed at. Outline enemies you shoot for a duration.
Stocked
Hold an additional Lethal and Tactical Equipment item and see a visible trajectory. Deal increased explosive damage from Equipment and Launchers. When pulled from a Loadout obtain full ammo reserves for your Primary and Secondary Weapon.
Incognito
Reduce the duration you are revealed on radar and the Hunter outline. You can only be danger pinged and you delay triggered traps.
Surveyor
On kill, briefly snapshot the nearby enemy squad and highlight enemies in last stand. Enemies appear on radar when activating a Buy Station or Contract.
Perk 2 Category (5)
Mountaineer
Reduces damage taken from falls avoiding stun and improves climb speeds. Can combat roll to further reduce fall damage.
Adaptive
Improved sprint to fire with full plates and movement speed when missing plates.
Scavenger
On kill, enemies drop a pack that supplies ammo, equipment, plates, and cash for your squad.
Drill Instructor
Improves your squad's sprint speed (does not stack). Sustained sprinting starts your health regen.
Deft Hands
Insert plates, use equipment, and capture crates faster. Gain a burst of speed on plate insertion.
Perk 3 Category (5)
Berserker
On kill, briefly improve ADS, weapon swap, and reload speeds.
Quick Fix
On kill or plate insertion, immediately start health regeneration.
Survivor
Begin regenerating health more quickly. Resistant to Flash, Concussion, and Shock equipment effects. Reduce non-Killstreak explosive damage and immune to knockdown.
Tempered
When refilling armor, it takes two plates instead of three.
Wingman
Your allies cost less to buy back. Redeploys do not produce a flare for enemies.
New Elite Perks (3)
What are Elite Perks?
Elite Perks add a fourth Perk slot to your Loadout, offering improved combat capabilities for your Operator. These special Perks are only discoverable within a match, found at Buy Stations, with additional ones through completing Supply Run Contracts. Only one Elite Perk can be equipped at a time.
Athleticism
Become more agile when strafing, crouching, and when in ADS. Reduced ADS penalties for jumping and sliding.
Phantom
Become undetectable from enemy recon Killstreaks, equipment, and Perks.
Fieldcraft
Rapidly regenerate armor and equipment for sustained combat.
Call of Duty: Warzone: Available Equipment
Season 1 changes up the available Equipment both in and out of your Loadout. Here’s what you can equip:
Tacticals (8)
Choose your Loadout Tacticals to utilize equipment that disrupts enemies, reveals their positions, or helps you move through combat.
Flash Grenade:
Blinds and deafens targets.
Smoke Grenade:
 Smoke screen that blocks vision and automated targeting systems. Weapon fire and explosions rip through the coverage.
Snapshot Grenade: 
Provides a momentary glimpse of enemies within the immediate area for your team.
Concussion Grenade: 
Disorients enemies, resulting in reduced reaction speeds and blurred vision.
Heartbeat Sensor: 
Battery-powered tablet that displays nearby enemy locations.
Shock Stick: 
Electrical device that sticks to surfaces, electrocutes enemies, destroys equipment, and causes vehicles to go haywire.
Stim: 
Military stimulant that immediately starts health regeneration, boost speed, and refreshes Tactical Sprint. Mitigates flash and stun effects.
Screamer Mine: 
Proximity-triggered mine that suppresses and marks all enemies in range.
Lethals (10)
Lethals:
 Select one of the following for your Loadout. Lethals and explosive and other high-damage equipment are used to eliminate enemies and destroy vehicles.
Frag Grenade:
Cookable fragmentation grenade.
Sticky Grenade:
Timed sticky grenade.
Drill Charge:
Thermal lance that burrows through surfaces before releasing an explosive round.
Proximity Mine:
Proximity-triggered explosive that launches and detonates in the air. Can also stick to vehicles that pass over it before exploding.
Trip Wire:
Thrown explosive trap that deploys a detonation wire upon landing. Multiple Trip Wires can link together, creating a connected network of explosives.
Thermite:
Sticky incendiary device that deals heavy damage to vehicles and armored targets.
Molotov Cocktail:
Improvised incendiary device that explodes on impact, leaving flammable pools in the area.
Claymore:
Proximity-activated explosive mine.
C4:
Large explosive that sticks to surfaces and can be detonated remotely or by double-tapping your trigger device.
Throwing Knife:
Retrievable knife that is lethal on impact to unarmored targets or the head.
Field Upgrades (13)
Field Upgrades are timed devices that can be found as scavenged loot or bought from Buy Stations in-match. These are not part of your Loadout. There are two types of Field Upgrades; New and Returning.
New Field Upgrades
 have not been seen in 
Call of Duty: Warzone
 before.
Returning Field Upgrades
 have been available in previous versions of 
Call of Duty: Warzone
.
Here’s what to look for:
Grabber Drone (New with MW4):
A remotely controlled flying drone with retractable claw.
Perk Supply Box (New with MW4):
Deploy a perk box for you and your team that provides a random perk.
Razor Wire (New with MW4):
Proximity-activated mine that ensnares enemies in a mesh of razor wire.
Redeploy Drone and Redeploy Drone Beacon (New with MW4):
 A marker you can throw or drop onto the ground that spawns a Redeploy Drone above, allowing for a team to redeploy. 
Hajin Massive Resurgence and Rally Point Modes only.
SAM Airburst Turret (New with MW4):
Portable missile turret that intercepts aerial threats.
Smoke Wall (New with MW4):
Hand-held rocket that emits a long trail of heavy smoke coverage.
Zipline Launchers (New with MW4):
Deploy a zipline to open additional routes of traversal.
Armor Box (Returning):
Deploy a box of armor plates for you and your teammates.
Munitions Box (Returning):
Deploy a box of ammo and equipment for you and your teammates.
Portable Radar (Returning):
Emits a periodic radar ping to detect nearby enemies.
Recon Drone (Returning):
Remote-controlled drone that has manual and auto-marking capabilities.
Trophy System (Returning):
Deployable defense system that destroys nearby explosive projectiles. Larger targets take multiple projectiles to counter.
Utility Box (Returning):
 Deploy a box of ammo, equipment, and armor plates for you and your teammates.
Killstreaks (9)
Killstreaks are powerful, single-use support assets you can find or purchase during a match, then deploy for a tactical advantage. These are also not part of your Loadout, and there are new and returning Killstreaks to acquire:
Aerial Scanner (New with MW4):
 Recon device that reveals enemies within a designated zone. Only available in Rally Point.
Artillery Beacon (New with MW4):
 Calls in heat-seeking missiles that track the deployed beacon.
Bomb Glider (New with MW4):
 Hand-held glider with an explosive payload.
Bombing Run (New with MW4):
 Calls in an aerial bombing run that bombards the target location twice.
Combat Drone (New with MW4):
 Remote controlled anti-personnel drone.
Impact Warhead (New with MW4):

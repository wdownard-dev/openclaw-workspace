Modern Warfare 4 is now live! Experience a brand-new Campaign, deploy across 20 Multiplayer maps, and drop into Hajin in the DMZ extraction mode.
Call of Duty®: Modern Warfare 4
 is now live worldwide! In the epic Campaign, Price is on the run, conflict has erupted on the Korean Peninsula, and a catastrophic weapon of war is on the verge of falling into the wrong hands. 
Deploy across 20 Multiplayer maps at launch, including 12 new 6v6 maps, 4 Gunfight maps, 3 Ground War maps, and the new Kill Block modular training facility. Experience grounded, precise combat built around fluid movement, player choice, and greater control in every engagement. 
In the DMZ extraction mode, operate as an off-the-books asset behind enemy lines, where every deployment into contested territory forces hard choices about which objectives to pursue, what to secure, and when to get out.

Starting today, support veterans with the Call of Duty Endowment (C.O.D.E.) Valor Pack for 
Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4
. The pack will be available at launch in Multiplayer and DMZ before it releases in 
Call of Duty: Warzone
™ with Season 1. 
Get the pack now
.
Created in collaboration with U.S. Marine Corps veteran and Medal of Honor recipient Kyle Carpenter, the C.O.D.E. Valor Pack brings the story you've seen throughout Forged in Valor into the game. Working closely with the Infinity Ward and Endowment teams, Cpl. Kyle (Ret.) helped shape a pack filled with personal references drawn from his life, service, recovery, and the mindset that continues to drive him forward.
The Valor Pack includes the following in-game items:
“Valor” Operator
“Relentless” Operator Skin
“Forged Resolve” M4 Assault Rifle Blueprint
“Still Standing” Rezi 12 Shotgun Blueprint
“Worth Fighting For” Combat Knife Melee Blueprint
“Nice Try” Weapon Charm
“Steel Talon” Weapon Sticker
“Forged in Valor” Calling Card
“Earned, Not Given” Emblem
“Blood, Sweat, & Sand” Loading Screen
1 Hour Double Level XP Token  
1 Hour Double Weapon XP Token 
"There are two types of courage: the courage to act and the courage to endure."
 – Kyle Carpenter
From phrases that have guided him through adversity to details inspired by real moments from his military journey, every item reflects the resilience, determination, and optimism that define his story.
Among the items is Kyle's favorite, the "Nice Try" Weapon Charm, a playful nod to the humor, attitude, and resilience that have become part of his story. While the pack honors remarkable acts of courage, it also captures the mantras that continue to drive Kyle forward today.
One hundred percent of Activision’s net proceeds will be donated to the Call of Duty Endowment. For those of you who don’t know, the Endowment helps U.S. and U.K. veterans find high-quality jobs after their service. Since their founding in 2009, they have placed more than 180,000+ veterans into great jobs.

Link Your Discord Account
We are adding new Discord features to 
Call of Duty
! By linking your account, you will have direct access to your Discord friends and have the ability to send invites directly from the game. As a thank you, players who link their accounts will receive a bonus Calling Card. 
For players who linked their Activision and Discord accounts during the Beta: with the launch of 
Modern Warfare 4
, you will need to link your account again to access these functionalities. Your bonus Calling Card will already be in your inventory, and you will also be granted 1-hour Double XP and 1 hour Double Weapon XP. 
Follow these steps to link (or re-link) your account:
Sign in to the Activision
 account used to play 
Call of Duty
.
Open Account Linking and select Discord.
Sign into the Discord account you want to link, and if you agree to link your account, hit authorize.
Return to 
Call of Duty
 and restart the game for the connection to take effect and the new features to become available.
If you're playing on PC, you can also follow the instructions within the game under the Discord tab in your Social menu.

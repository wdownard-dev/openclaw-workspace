How Does the Modern Warfare 4 Battle Pass Work?
The plan is straightforward: Simply play 
Modern Warfare 4
 Multiplayer, DMZ, or 
Call of Duty®: Warzone™,
 earning XP as you go, which levels up your Battle Pass from Tier 1 through 100.
Simply earn Tiers through playing the game.
Battle Pass owners also receive a 10% Battle Pass XP Boost for the entire season, as a reward for purchasing the Battle Pass.
Optionally use Tier Skip Tokens with COD Points to instantly claim any unlocked rewards.*
Optionally use Battle Pass 2XP Tokens you’ve acquired to quicken progress throughout the Battle Pass. Active XP Tokens are paused outside of matches.
Auto Catch-up:
 Players who fall behind in the Battle Pass are automatically caught up to the Tier reward matching the current day of the season, up to day 50.
*Tier Skip Tokens:
 Tier Skip Tokens allow you to access higher level rewards in the Battle Pass, unlocking content ahead of the next tier on the track. Players who purchase the Battle Pass Bundle can use their Tier Skip Tokens to access any of the 10 unlocked tiers ahead of their current position, while BlackCell users can grab rewards from up to 20 tiers ahead. Spending a Tier Skip Token on a higher tier reward will not change your in-progress position on the Battle Pass.

Season 1: Main Battle Pass and BlackCell Operators
Get more intel on featured Season 1 Operators Blix and XERO, who both excel in the art of stealth and precision.
Blix (Battle Pass, Launch)
Battle Pass Instant Reward
A former South Korean Special Warfare Command operator, Blix was captured during a covert border mission and held for years. After surviving imprisonment, he was transferred through a network of foreign captives and deployed into proxy conflicts.
War reshaped his view of combat into an absolute doctrine. He began tattooing survival codes and tactical principles across his skin, turning himself into a living archive. His mechanical efficiency and indifference to pain drew the attention of masked mercenary leader Nikto, who recruited him as a premier operative. Now in the Exclusion Zone, Blix carries out each objective with silent, disciplined precision.
XERO (BlackCell, Launch)
BlackCell Instant Reward
BlackCell has no record of XERO before a classified development program involving behavioral conditioning, synthetic identities, and experimental combat training. His designation traces back to X-0, an internal prototype number later replaced by a single name: XERO.
Field personnel describe him as unnervingly controlled, with flashes of memory tied to places and people absent from his official history. BlackCell refuses to explain them. Some believe his identity was erased; others suspect he was assembled from failed subjects. One theory is more disturbing: BlackCell created XERO’s past where none existed.
Now deployed when an operation requires complete deniability, XERO remains one of BlackCell’s most closely controlled assets.

 DMZ-Related Store Content: DMZ+ Operators and Related Bundles
At the start of Season 1, certain Operator Skins – available via the 
Modern Warfare 4 
Vault Edition or by purchasing specific Store Bundles – will have a specific Permanent Prestige Trait associated with them. These are known as DMZ+ Operators.
Only the purchased Operator Skin for the DMZ+ Operator will have access to this specific Prestige Trait.
As a reminder, earned Prestige Traits are applicable to all the owned Operator Skins under that selected Operator.
For example, if you own the 
Modern Warfare 4 
Vault Edition, you have access to the “Ghost” Operator Skin within the Hostile Alliance Operator Pack and gain access to the “Field Crafting Pro” Prestige Trait for this skin only.
If you equip the “Golden Wraith” Operator Skin for Ghost after purchasing the “Bonechiller II” Store Bundle, you gain access to the Prestige Trait tied to that skin only (in this case, “Free Revive Pro”).
If you switch to another Ghost skin, you no longer have the purchased DMZ+ Prestige Trait associated with the previous Skin.
A DMZ Operator can never have more than three Prestige Traits at a time. If you already have three earned Prestige Traits on an Operator and gain access to a DMZ+ Trait, you must swap out one of your existing Prestige Traits if you wish to use the Prestige Trait tied to the specific Operator Skin.

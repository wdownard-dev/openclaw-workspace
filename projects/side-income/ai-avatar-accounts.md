## 1. How the money is made

- **TikTok Creator Rewards** (formerly Creativity Program Beta). Primary terms (last updated 2026-07-20): 18+, at least 10,000 authentic followers, at least 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, a personal account in good standing, a payment account in the creator's own name, and only videos of at least 1 minute. TikTok may cap rewards and excludes "artificially produced" metrics. No rate is published. https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/creator-rewards-program-us/en
- **Rate (unverified, vendor sources):** ShortSync says about $0.40–$1.20 per 1,000 qualified views (https://www.shortsync.app/resources/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026). Gemlist says about $0.40–$1.00, "creator-reported, no official rate" (https://www.gemlist.io/blog/tiktok-creator-rewards-requirements). Both are tool or education sellers, so treat them as commercial sources.
- **Brand deals, TikTok Shop affiliate, selling courses or products:** not verified. I did not retrieve TikTok Shop affiliate requirements. The "faceless AI influencer" income material I found is mostly course or tool marketing.

## 2. Platform rules (verified where stated)

- **TikTok AI-generated content:** creators must label AI-generated or significantly edited content showing realistic-looking scenes or people. Unlabeled content "may be removed, restricted, or labeled." https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity
- **TikTok unoriginal content:** content "is ineligible for the FYF [For You feed] if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new." Same page. Reach is lost even when the video stays up.
- **TikTok impersonation and multiple accounts:** impersonation is prohibited. Multiple accounts are allowed for fan or creative use, "but not to deceive others or break" rules. Same page.
- **Instagram / Meta AI labels and unoriginal-content demonetization:** **unverified.** I could not reach Meta's policy pages in this run. Treat current Instagram rules as unknown until checked.

## 4. Realistic outcomes

- The Creator Rewards gate (10,000 followers and 100,000 views in 30 days) is the hard constraint. The time a new faceless account needs to reach it is **not sourced**. My estimate is months, with low odds, and it depends on niche and luck.
- Failure modes from the sources above: unlabeled AI people removed or restricted; reused or templated videos dropped from the For You feed; inflated metrics excluded from rewards; account-level penalties; and policy changes, since TikTok's terms say rewards may be modified or ceased.
- Saturation: no data gathered.

## 6. Verdict

The promo is hype. Platform money is small, discretionary, and gated behind audience thresholds. The faceless-AI route adds labeling and originality risk, and HeyGen's free tier cannot be monetized. **Do not start an income stream on this now.** If William wants to test it, use the cheapest version below.

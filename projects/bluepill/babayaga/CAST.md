## Characters (named or recurring people and creatures)
1. **Baba Yaga**: tall hunched old woman, grey hair, long nose, dark ash-stained shawl; one bony leg shown as a thin pale limb (Afanasyev repertoire; marker: the bone leg). Costume marker: dark shawl. Cards: neutral, sniffing, sweeping with broom, laughing-snarling.
2. **Vasilisa**: eight years old, the smallest figure in every frame, plain light kerchief, simple sarafan, carries the doll. Marker: kerchief.
3. **Vasilisa's mother** (deathbed): pale, in bed, blessing hand raised; appears in two shots only.
4. **Merchant father**: brief, in a long coat; leaves for a trip. Optional card; one shot.
5. **Stepmother**: stern, wide, dark red dress, hair covered, kept lit from below by the single candle. Marker: red dress.
6. **Stepsister A**: broad, in a patterned blouse, snuffs the candle.
7. **Stepsister B**: matching pair with A, taller, distinct kerchief colour so the two read as two.
8. **The doll**: small figure with a plain face, tiny red dress, sits in Vasilisa's pocket or lap; eats a crumb in two shots. Marker: red dress, round head.
9. **White Rider**: all white, white horse, light rim on a dawn sky. Spoken label "my bright Day".
10. **Red Rider**: all red, red horse, rising sun behind. "my red Sun".
11. **Black Rider**: all black, black horse, almost lost against night; only the horse's eye reads. "my dark Night".
12. **The Hands (three pairs)**: six pale disembodied hands carrying sacks of grain. Hands only; no arms in narration (ours: hands without bodies).
13. **Girl of tale 2**: a girl in a plain sarafan, tie-a-ribbon-on-the-birch scene; separate card from Vasilisa so the two tales do not blur.
14. **Stepmother of tale 2**: reuse stepmother card with variant clothing, or a new card (decision at library stage).
15. **Real aunt (tale 2)**: kind older woman at a table; one shot.
16. **Cat (Baba Yaga's)**: black cat, big eyes; takes the meat, gives the comb and towel.
17. **Dog (Baba Yaga's)**: thin yard dog; takes the bread.
18. **Birch**: a white-trunked birch with a ribbon tied to a branch; "character" with a face-free trunk, two branches as arms.
19. **The Gate (tale 2 servant)**: a plain wooden yard gate with oiled hinges; distinct from the skull gate.
20. **Servant maids** (Baba Yaga's household, tale 2): two faceless women in grey, sweeping; one shot, optional.

# Props

One master per object on plain cream (the ship and its beached/sea variants live with the places); derived states are edits of the master.

- `boulder.png`: The cave boulder: a huge round grey boulder of cave-door size. Derived from a crop of places/cave_exterior/day_wide.png (it IS the exterior boulder).
- `ship.png`: MASTER ship, side view, sail up: dark teal hull with a gold Greek meander band along the top, a row of seven round oar holes below it, carved curled prow with a painted eye, single mast, square sail with vertical deep-blue and white stripes.
- `ship_furled.png`: Same ship, sail furled: the stripes stay visible wrapped on the yard. Derived from ship.png.
- `stake_raw.png`: Stake master: a raw olive trunk, flat-cut ends.
- `stake_sharp.png`: Stake state 2: the same trunk sharpened to a point. Derived from stake_raw.png.
- `stake_fire.png`: Stake state 3: the same trunk, sharpened, red-orange fire-hardened tip. Derived from stake_raw.png.
- `stake.png`: The three stake states stacked in one image (composited in code from stake_raw / stake_sharp / stake_fire).
- `wine.png`: A dark goatskin wine bag with a tied neck beside a shallow clay bowl of dark red wine.
- `torch.png`: A wooden torch: rough shaft, pitch-soaked cloth head, bright orange flame (the Abel sailor's torch).
- `olive_club.png`: A rough gnarled olive-wood club with bark and a worn grip.
- `cheese_set.png`: Three big round pale-yellow cheese wheels stacked, a round wicker basket with a striped woollen cloth, and a tall two-handled stone water jar (also the woven basket and stone jar of the script). Matches the cave interior.
- `lyre.png`: A Greek lyre: tortoise-shell body, two curved wooden arms with a crossbar, seven strings.
- `scroll_quill.png`: A partly unrolled blank papyrus scroll with a white quill and a clay inkwell.
- `book_pile.png`: A tall wobbling pile of old leather-bound books and manuscripts in deep blue, burgundy, green and ochre. No titles.
- `brass_lamp.png`: A polished brass oil lamp with curved spout and a curling sheet of blank paper on a small rug-covered market table.
- `elephant_skull.png`: Elephant skull, FRONT view: pale bone, exactly ONE big round hole in the centre of the forehead, NO eye sockets, two curved tusks.
- `elephant_skull_in_clay.png`: The same skull front view half embedded in a rough orange-brown clay wall (derived from elephant_skull).
- `dwarf_elephant.png`: A small dwarf elephant, pony-sized, standing in side view, grey wrinkled skin, short tusks.
- `mule_pair.png`: Two brown mules in harness straining forward in side view, ropes to a huge rope-bound sandstone block at left.
- `ox_cart.png`: A small ancient wooden ox-cart with two solid wheels and plank bed, a pair of pale oxen in front, side view (wagons/ox-carts for scale).
- `axe_tongs_trough.png`: A glowing hot bronze axe head in iron tongs over a wooden water trough with steam. Objects only, no hands (smith simile).
- `map_antique.png`: Antique sandstone-and-gold parchment map: Mediterranean-style coastline and islands in pale teal, gold border, compass rose, small painted longship, caravel and onion-domed church. No text.
- `map_rolled_blank.png`: The same parchment rolled up and tied with a cord, one corner unrolled showing blank parchment (derived from map_antique; the bard's rolled-up blank map).
- `gulls.png`: Five white seagulls with grey wings and yellow beaks in flight, varied poses.
- `anvil_bolt.png`: A dark iron anvil on a stump, forging hammer, and tongs holding a glowing white-gold zigzag thunderbolt.
- `straw_bowls.png`: A heap of straw and hay, four straws in a fan (no hand), a row of three empty shallow clay bowls and one round wooden bowl.
- `ram.png`: MASTER ram: a big woolly ram with huge curled gold-brown horns and thick cream fleece hanging to its knees. The character sheet characters/ram is derived from it.

Prop/place consistency: the boulder prop is cut from the cave exterior master; the ship in places/beach_and_ship is the props/ship.png ship; the interior boulder close-up is a crop of the interior master.

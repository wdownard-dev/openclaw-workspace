# Reference channel: Unknown Frequencies (@unknownfrequencies-tv), reviewed 2026-11-11

Shared by William as the animation style he likes. Data from the YouTube API and yt-dlp; frames from "The ENTIRE Story of The Odyssey Explained" (13 min, 164 cuts, about one cut per 5 s) and the 24 thumbnails.

## Channel facts [V]
- 24 videos, all published 2026-02-17 to 2026-11-10 (about one every 11 days); channel created 2024-10-26.
- 92.3k subscribers, 12.73M total views (about 138 views per subscriber, so low subscribe conversion).
- Length 9-18 min (540-1086 s). Channel description: "I'm using Paint to explain history", researched from public sources, no glorification of violence.
- Format families: "The ENTIRE History/Story of ___ Explained" (WW1, WW2, Cold War, Vietnam, Canada, Odyssey, Trojan War, Greek Mythology, Achilles), "Your Life as ___ / POV: You're ___ / What It Was Like to be Every ___ Rank" soldier-life videos, plus a few "evolution of" and "every ___ explained".
- Mythology videos: Odyssey 1.67M, Greek Mythology 795k, Trojan War 474k, Achilles 371k, Evolution of the Ancient Greek Soldier 96k.
- Best videos overall: WW2 2.25M, Odyssey 1.67M, German Army Ranks 1.14M, Vietnam War 1.03M. Weakest: snipers (9k, newest), WW2 holdouts 31k, Vietnam soldier life 66k.

## Visual style (observed)
- Simple, consistent cartoon characters (round white heads, dot eyes, tiny mouths, one distinct skull-faced soldier) composited over detailed painterly illustrated backgrounds: caves, ships, palace halls, storm seas, maps with route arrows. The backgrounds look far more polished than hand-made Paint work and are very likely AI-generated illustrations (not verified; the channel says it uses Paint).
- Characters keep the same face, helmet, beard and costume across shots; side characters (Zeus, Athena, Polyphemus) are drawn in the same cartoon head style.
- Big hand-lettered title cards in one font and one accent colour (rust red on white or dark), single-word impact captions ("Cyclops!", "Tie me tighter!"), counts ("12 ships, 600 men", "108 suitors"), arrows pointing at things, parchment scrolls for morals.
- Alternating shot types: full illustrated scene, character-on-white cutaway, inset picture-in-picture, map with route arrows.
- Thumbnails: two or three characters facing off over a map or setting, big title and "IN 13 MINUTES" label; "YOU" arrow and "DAY 5" labels for POV videos. Strongly templated across the channel.

## What it means for Blue Pill
- This is within reach of our planned pipeline: a locked character kit plus generated backgrounds plus Remotion overlays for text, arrows and maps. It needs more unique background images per video than the Paint Explainer format (probably 80-150), so image cost is higher, my estimate $5-12 per video, to be priced.
- Mythology works for them, with 370k-1.67M views per video on a 92k-sub channel even though Greek content is crowded (median 59k in our sample). Production quality and a clear "ENTIRE story in N minutes" promise beat saturation.
- Their cadence (about 2-3 videos a month) is a realistic target for the first months.
- Cautions: subscriber conversion is low; their thumbnail system is heavily templated (our version must be distinct and each video needs its own story to stay within the inauthentic-content policy); we should not copy their characters, fonts or thumbnails.

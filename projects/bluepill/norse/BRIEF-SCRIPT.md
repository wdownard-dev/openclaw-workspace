One straight chronological story, the Eddas as the spine, Snorri as the closing "where did this come from" chapter. Same channel, same format, same length as video 1 so the lane comparison is clean.

Requirements:
- 1,450-1,650 spoken words, 6-8 named chapters, 9:30-10:00 at 150 wpm. Mean 10-12 words per sentence, 12-14 sentences a minute, 0.3-0.5 questions a minute.
- Chronological spine: Ymir and Audhumla, the making of the world from Ymir's body, Odin and the three brothers, Yggdrasil and the nine worlds (keep it light), Loki and his three children (Fenrir, Jormungandr, Hel), binding of Fenrir (Tyr's hand), Thor and Jormungandr (the fishing trip), Baldr's death and Loki's punishment, Ragnarok and the world reborn. Closing chapter: where the myth comes from (oral tradition, Codex Regius, Snorri Sturluson about 1220, a Christian Icelander writing it down; the sources disagree in places; say so once).
- Hook (first 30 s): bold claim with a NUMBER, two or three escalating facts, a collapse line, then the question the video answers. Number-first.
- Chapter titles phrased as story claims (e.g. "The Wolf Everyone Agreed to Chain"), not topic labels.
- Sources: public domain only. Poetic Edda (Bellows 1923, Thorpe 1866), Prose Edda (Brodeur 1916). Where the Eddas disagree, pick one version and mention the other once in NOTES. Never invent a quote and present it as a source. Dialogue is "ours" and flagged as such.
- Every beat has a SHOT LINE and a SCENE STATE line (location, time of day, who is present and how many, action direction). Describe only who is present. No unfilmable shot lines. Keep cast small per beat. Bold, simple character features (Odin: one eye, wide hat; Thor: red beard, hammer; Loki: ... pick bold tells) because small features vanish in wide shots.
- No text cards, no on-screen text, no chapter bands (PROCESS rule 8).
- Stylised violence only; general-audience.

# WARDROBE.md (Norse, video 2): text checkpoint before any image is generated

Status: DRAFT for William. Text only; no images, audio or spend. Beat ids and `state:` variants come from `norse/SCRIPT-N-LOCKED.md`; cast, scale line and prop list from `norse/SCRIPT-N-NOTES.md`; card spec and rule 21 from `PROCESS.md`.

**Locator caveat.** Edda locators below (Gylf = Gylfaginning, Brodeur 1916 chapter numbers as in `SCRIPT-N-NOTES.md`; Voluspa / Grimnismal / Lokasenna etc. = Bellows 1923 stanza numbers) are written from memory of the texts, same as the notes. This pass did NOT read them (sacred-texts.com is blocked from this host and no web read was done). Each is marked `(verify)` where I am less than sure. The fact-check pass owns confirming them; none of them changes a costume. Colours are OUR design: no source gives Loki a green cloak or Tyr a tunic colour. Where a tell is traditional I say so; where it is ours I say "ours".

**Style rule for every identity line.** Round cream face, two small black dot eyes, no nose, thick dark ink outline, flat warm shading, simple stick-figure body with chunky hands (Odysseus CHARACTER.md and STYLE.txt). Exceptions are deliberate and listed (Odin's socket, Hod's ring eyes). Plain cream background on the card, no text, no labels on the figure.

### 1.3 Pairwise confusion check (what I changed)

Method: for every shot where two or more figures share a frame (the `state:` lines), I compared main colour and silhouette.

| Pair (shared beats) | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Odin vs Tyr (N-070–079, N-144; both in blue) | Both blue-clothed men in a 4-figure wide shot | Tyr tunic from blue to dark teal; Tyr bare-headed, shield on the left arm |
| Vili, Ve vs Ask, Embla (N-035–038; green vs leaf-green) | The brothers' green cloak matches the pair's tunics | Vili from green to deep purple; Ve stays yellow |
| Vidar vs Modi and Magni (N-158; dark red vs red capes) | Vidar's dark red and the boys' red capes in one shot | Vidar from dark red to dark olive |

| Odin vs Snorri | Dark-blue robe + quill vs dark-blue cloak | Snorri maroon with a fur collar, no hat, quill |
| Snorri vs the scribe | Tonsure on Snorri would read as a monk | Snorri keeps hair; the scribe gets the tonsure and habit |
| Frigg vs Odin | Both blue | They never share a beat. Frigg lighter sky-blue, headdress, braid. No change forced |

## 5. Risks (what could still read wrong at 540p, and what to look at first)

1. **Odin's missing eye.** A dark socket on a dot-eyed stick face can read as a wink, a smudge or an eye-patch. This is the story's central image and 55 beats depend on it. Look at the Odin face close-up first. Fallback: a plain black patch, one tweak.
2. **Hod's ring eyes** (N-118) may read as "wide-eyed" rather than blind. Everyone else has dot eyes, so the difference is visible but subtle. If it fails: a pale-grey cloth band across the eyes.
3. **Thor's disguise.** If the hood hides too much, nothing links the disguised youth to Thor for 20 beats, and the viewer may think a stranger arrived. The red cuffs, boots and belt-hammer are the link; check the disguise card against the Thor card side by side.
4. **Tyr's stump.** A white bandage on the right sleeve is small. In 4-figure wide shots (N-078–079, N-144) the stump is a few pixels. Mitigation: the locked N-078 shot already puts the stump in a wide shot; the plate prompt must say "right sleeve ends in a white bandaged stump, arm hanging". Check the 540p crop of N-078 first among plates.
5. **Fenrir's stages.** Cub, young, huge, bound and bound-with-sword are five images of one wolf; drift in the mane and eye colour is the usual failure. The five derive from one card by edit.
6. **Scale.** Hymir 3.0 in a boat with Thor, and the giantess 3.0 in a hut. Both need a bigger boat and a bigger cave than the notes assume (done above). Ymir at 4.0 and Surt at 4.0 need cropped framing.
7. **Place variants under heavy lighting changes.** `new_world_snow`, `new_world_burning` and `open_sea_naglfar` change a lot; edge correlation may fall below 0.6. If so, regenerate those three from the master prompt rather than patching.
8. **Reused Ask and Embla as Lif and Lifthrasir** (N-160–161). Same faces and tunics read as "the same two" which is the story's point, but a viewer may think it is Ask and Embla surviving. Acceptable.
9. **Snorri's cross** at N-168 is tiny. If it vanishes the beat still works (a man writing), but the "Christian" line leans on it. Option: a larger wooden cross hung in the study background instead (place variant).
10. **Heimdall vs Baldr.** Both pale; Baldr has the gold glow, Heimdall pure white with a gold horn. They never share a beat.
11. **Armour at N-144–146** if `odin_armed` is rejected.
12. **Locators** are unverified (caveat above).

What William should look at first: (1) Odin's face close-up, (2) the Tyr pair (hand and stump) next to Odin and Thor, (3) the Thor and thor_disguise pair, (4) Hod's face, (5) the Fenrir stage row.

## P8 The Christian Who Saved The Gods (beats 162-174, 800 chars)

So how do we know any of this?

None of this was written down when the poems were first made.

For generations it was recited from memory by poets.

Most of the poems survive in one great book, the Codex Regius, from about twelve seventy.

It was written in Iceland, by Christians, about gods nobody worshipped anymore.

Around twelve twenty, an Icelandic chieftain named Snorry Sturloosun put together a handbook for poets.

Snorri was a Christian too, and his book opens with Adam and Eve, then says the gods were once men from Asia.

Not every source agrees.

In one poem, the makers of the first people have different names.

When they disagree, we follow Snorri.

He was killed in twelve forty-one, on the orders of a Norwegian king.

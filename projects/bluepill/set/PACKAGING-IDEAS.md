# PACKAGING-IDEAS.md: Set (video 3), Stage 1

Rules: under 60 characters, no "ENTIRE" (William, 2026-11-14), no hype words (VOICE.md). Character counts include spaces. Thumbnails go through the card method (PROCESS Stage 10: labelled library cards + identity lines as references, checklist audit), flat style, characters large, one eye-catching read at 320x180.

## Titles (pick first)
1. **Set: Why Egypt Turned Its Strongest God Into a Villain** (54). Pick. States the angle as a question the viewer wants answered, carries "Set" and "Egypt" for search, and "strongest god" tells a stranger he was not a minor figure. Honest to the content (beats 5-21).
2. The Murderer Who Guarded the Sun (33). Pure picture of the contradiction; short and strong on mobile; no keyword, so weaker for search.
3. Pharaohs Named Themselves After Osiris's Murderer (49). Number-free hook built on beat 11; requires viewers to know Osiris, so narrower.
4. The Egyptian God Who Killed His Brother, Then Saved the Sun (59). Two actions, both specific; at the character limit, may truncate on mobile.
5. Set: The Villain Egypt Built Over 2,000 Years (45). Matches the close line; calm tone, lower curiosity.
6. Why Seti Was Named After the God of Murder (41). Odd-fact hook, but "god of murder" overstates Set (he is god of storm, desert, foreigners).

A/B suggestion: 1 vs 2 if Test & Compare is available.

## Thumbnails (card method, 1280x720)
**A (pick): "SAME GOD."** Split frame. Left half: Set (red-brown, long snout, tall square ears) beside a closed painted chest, dark blue night. Right half: the same Set at the prow of a golden sun-barque, spear raised, spearing a coiled serpent, orange sun behind. Big text "SAME GOD." across the seam. Reads at 320x180 as two contrasting jobs, one figure; needs Set and Apophis cards and a barque plate.
**B: "NAMED AFTER HIM."** A large royal cartouche reading Seti with the Set animal glyph in front, Set's head looming behind it in ink blue and gold. Text "KINGS NAMED THEMSELVES AFTER THE MURDERER" (shorten to "NAMED AFTER HIM"). Strong curiosity, weaker picture.
**C: "ERASED."** Set's face half carved into a temple wall, a hand with a chisel gouging it out, stone dust, text "ERASED". Dramatic, matches H7, hides the guardian side of the angle.

## 1. How characters are drawn

**There are three character tiers, not one style** [V]. This corrects our earlier note ("round white heads, dot eyes"): that describes only the minor tier.

| Tier | Who | How drawn |
|---|---|---|
| Hero tier | Odysseus' soldiers, the heroic "narrator-surrogate" (gold Corinthian helmet with red crest, red cape, gold breastplate) | White skull-like face with two black eye sockets and an open black mouth, large head on a small body, heavily detailed painted armour with highlights and a dark outline. Used as the recurring "face" in cutaway shots on plain white [V]. |
| Everyman tier | Odysseus (as a bearded man with a big dark curly-hair mass), Penelope, Telemachus, Athena, Aeolus, Tiresias, Laertes' era figures | Round white or cream head, two dot eyes, small line eyebrows, one-line mouth, hair as a big dark scribbled mass, beard as a dark fill, plain grey cuirass and skirt. Faces are blank but eyebrows and mouth carry acting [V]. Thin black pen outline, slightly wobbly [V]. Colour: grey, cream, purple, rust, little saturation. |
| Monster/god tier | Polyphemus, the giants (Laestrygonians), Scylla, Charybdis, Poseidon (full body), the Sun cattle, Helios | Drawn **realistically / semi-realistically**: muscular anatomy, scars, blood, hair strands, fur loincloth, painted shading. Polyphemus has a human-like face with ONE eye, a heavy brow, grimace and blood (a "stabbed eye" with an X shape in a later shot) [V]. These look like AI or hand-painted illustration, not the Paint-doodle style. |

**Other details** [V]
- Head shape: round (everyman) or slightly oval with chin (Penelope, Athena). Head about a third of body height or larger in cutaways; body is a small plain torso with stick arms and legs, small hands without fingers detail on everyman figures [V].
- Line weight: medium to thick black outline on characters, uniform, slightly wobbly; thinner or absent on painted backgrounds. White "sticker" outline around cutaways on dark backgrounds [V f_21].
- Palette: restrained earthy: cream, parchment, rust red, gold, dusty blue, olive. Rust red (the accent colour of the title lettering) shows up in capes, crests and text [V]. Dark night and cave scenes drop to near-black with orange firelight [V].
- Shading: characters are flat colour with minimal shading; armour has simple highlights; monsters are fully modelled [V]. Backgrounds are modelled with light, glow and texture [V].
- Texture: backgrounds show painterly/paper-grain texture and soft vignettes; characters have none [V].
- Costume detail: the high-detail items are helmets, breastplates and capes (gold gradient, stitched leather straps); mundane characters wear plain tunics. Props are painted objects (amphorae, cheese wheels, sacks of wind, the bow, a loom) [V].

## 2. Acting and posing [V]
- Faces act through eyebrows and mouth: worried brows, crying with blue tear streaks, shouting open mouth, skeptical flat mouth [V f_03, f_29, f_31]. Several characters have a **mid-size exaggeration** (tear streaks, sweat or anger lines) but few emoji marks.
- Bodies are mostly front or three-quarter standing with one arm gesturing (pointing, shrug, palms up) [V]. Large cutaway shots show the character from the waist up on white and a single raised-finger or shrug pose [V f_07, f_31, f_35].
- Groups: several identical soldiers in a row on a ship deck, each with a different expression (suspicious, scared), mass crowds of tiny soldiers in the cave [V f_08, f_16, f_24].
- Side-by-side pairing for dialogue with speech bubbles or lettered quotes [V f_03, f_15].
- Continuity: the same face and costume in every appearance; different poses are separate drawings [V, E].

## 3. Backgrounds [V]
- Painterly, detailed, light-modelled: map of the Mediterranean with relief shading and route arrows, cave interiors with cheese wheels and lamps, ship decks with rigging, seaside beaches, palace rooms, pastoral meadows with a golden sun.
- Slightly stylised realism, warm lighting, dramatic vignettes in night and cave shots. Looks AI-generated, with a cartoon-leaning finish [E].
- White-background cutaways alternate with illustrated scenes [V].
- Maps are the connective tissue: 7 of 40 sampled frames are map shots with a travel arrow and place labels [V].

## 4. Cadence [V/E]
- 164 cuts in 13 minutes (earlier note), about 12.6 cuts per minute, a new image every 4 to 6 s [V from REF note, E for feel].
- In our 40 frames at 19 s spacing, 0 were duplicates of the previous frame, so new compositions are the norm [V]. Cutaway shots (a face on white) are reused [V f_07, f_31, f_35 are three different poses of the same helmet figure].

## 5. Text and captions [V]
- Hand-drawn, high-contrast, Greek-key-inspired caps lettering in rust red or white/black, huge on title cards, with one word in an accent colour (`SON OF LAERTES`) [V].
- Subtitles on screen in thin serif or hand font, bottom of frame, word-by-word [V f_38].
- Counts and tags (`12 SHIPS`, `9 DAYS`, `108 SUITORS DEAD`) used as impact text, not as shouted full-screen slogans [V].
- Speech bubbles are just hand-lettered text with a thin curved tail line [V].

## 7. Style principles to extract (not to copy)
1. **Tiered fidelity.** The cast is not one level of detail: minor figures are simple, the hero and monsters get high detail. Contrast is how the viewer reads importance. [E]
2. **Detailed costume, simple face.** The polish is in armour, props, texture. Faces are cheap but readable through brows and mouth.
3. **Humans are ordinary-proportioned stick bodies; monsters and gods are realistic and large.** Scale and rendering contrast sell menace, so the Cyclops looks dangerous, not cute.
4. **Restrained palette with one accent colour** (rust red) shared by lettering, capes and plumes.
5. **Painterly backgrounds, flat characters on top**, but characters have an outline and a white sticker edge on dark scenes, which makes them pop.
6. **Acting by expression sets and posed variants** (shrug, point, cry), not by animation.
7. **Humour comes from blank faces in grim situations**, and the grim moments (blood, scars) are drawn straight. [E]

## 8. What this means for our "childish" problem
- The ref does not use one soft kids-mascot look. It pairs deadpan simple faces with grown-up detail and gore-level monsters. Our v2 cast is soft round mascots on a sunny palette: that is the gap. [E]
- The reference's round heads are only in the mid tier; their hero tier and monsters are what make it feel grown up. [E]

# SCRIPT-V2.md: "The Cyclops Case File" (working title; Project Blue Pill, video 1, story-first rewrite)

Written 2026-11-12 for The Myth Ledger. Nothing recorded, generated, uploaded or sent. Recipe: `RECIPE-SCRIPT.md`. Facts: `../RESEARCH-V1.md` (ids A1.., B1.., C1.., D1..) plus six details I newly verified in Butler's Book 9 (N1-N6 below). Metrics script: `scripts/metrics.py`.

## How to read this file
- Each beat is `**NN · SPEAKER** spoken line`, then a `visual` note and a `trace` note. Speakers: NARR narrator (George), ODY Odysseus, POLY Polyphemus, CREW the crew (also the made-up sailor in the cold open), CYC2 the other Cyclopes. TEXT-CHIP lines are on-screen only and are never spoken.
- Visual grammar (from the reference): hard cuts only; a new picture or pop-in roughly every sentence or two; painted or inked scenes for the cave; bright white cutaways with sprites for performed dialogue; stamps, counters and 1-3 word labels instead of text tables; no burned-in captions on story shots; no crossfades.
- Trace: research id plus label. **V** verified in a text I read; **E** inference or paraphrase of a secondary source; **U** not used. "(gag)" means the line is our joke and carries no factual claim. "(dramatised)" means invented dialogue after Homer, covered by the one disclosure at beat 11. The sailor's two lines are ours and sit inside "Picture a sailor", which is an explicit imagining.
- **New verified details** (Butler prose translation, https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1727, read this session, Book 9): **N1** Odysseus and four men chosen by lot carry the stake; he hides it under dung. **N2** Polyphemus speaks to the ram: "My good ram... you lag last of all. Is it because you know your master has lost his eye?" **N3** Departure sequence: first jeer, a mountaintop thrown just in front of the ship, the crew beg ("he has thrown one rock at us already"), the second shout with the name; then Polyphemus recalls Telemus's prophecy ("a little insignificant weakling"), offers presents and a word to Neptune, and Odysseus answers that it will take more than Neptune to cure that eye. **N4** The prayer includes "late and in sore plight after losing all his men" before the bracketed lines. **N5** Maron's twelve jars are "unblended"; Maron himself mixed twenty parts water to one of wine. **N6** Polyphemus's first words ("Strangers, who are you?... traders, or... rovers?"); Odysseus asks for hospitality in Zeus's name; Polyphemus answers that Cyclopes do not care about Zeus. Also read: Polyphemus's question about where the ship is moored, the first-night supper, and the breakfast and evening meals that make six.

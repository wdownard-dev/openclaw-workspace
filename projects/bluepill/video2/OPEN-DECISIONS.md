## 1. Voices: four voices or George only
- **Recommend: four voices.** George narrates; Odysseus; Polyphemus; and one shared "everyone else" voice for the crew, the sailor and the other Cyclopes (they never share a scene).
- Why: V3 has 35 character lines in 19 exchanges, including three-way back-and-forths (Nobody, 28-33; the neighbours, 37-39; the sailor's questions, 64-75). With George playing both sides, listeners lose who is talking, and the sprites cannot swap on a voice change. Cost: three more voices to audition and level-match.
- Fallback: George for everything, with performance notes. Middle option: George plays Odysseus, one deep voice plays Polyphemus, one more voice plays the rest (three voices).
- Decide with a 30-second test: beats 28-33, 44 (the ram) and 64-68 (the sailor) in both setups. The one- and two-word lines ("Nope.", "Ah.", "Sheep.", "Rude.", "Nobody.") are the ones to listen for. If a listener can follow the Nobody exchange with George alone, go George-only.

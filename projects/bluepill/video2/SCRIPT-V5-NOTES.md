## Dropped from V4, and why
- The case board, all four stamps, CASE OPEN, IMAGINED, and every source chip: the recipe's biggest finding is that evidence apparatus is what makes V4 feel overloaded.
- All voiced character lines and the dialogue disclosure (V4 beat 04): narrator only, so there is nothing to disclose; Butler's wording is quoted only where it is verified.
- Gags and bits: the gift basket, "Ah.", "I am honoured.", "Rude.", "I keep sheep.", the Zeus/forge callbacks, the Hesiod "careers" line, "Where's the nose?", "Which ancient book says that?", and the optional board pings. They belong to the punchy lane.
- The Mayor/Discover material (V4 70-73): the Alexander-and-elephants sentence and both Mayor quotes. The story needs the hedge, not the argument; Abel is hedged as "imagined", "thought it might", "a modern claim, not ancient evidence".
- Colavito, Empedocles, Aguirre and Buxton, Burkert, Callimachus, Thucydides, Euripides, Theocritus, Aeolian forges, the Lion Gate lions as a separate beat, "22 wagons vs 2 mules": too many authors for a straight story. Hesiod stays because Homer's silence on the eye needs the one line that gives it.
- Hackman's catalogue is kept (221 versions, Iceland, Portugal, Russia, the Nights) with "many scholars" for its age; the "So I was not the first" gag and the "PROBABLY OLDER" stamp are gone.
- The four-suspect verdict structure and "Possible. Provable? No." lines. In V5 the origin ideas are told as story, with one closing line, "None of them can be proved."
- The "next time" tease and the "giant has a father" sting: V5 ends on "Nobody."

## The 5 places I am least sure of
1. **Added narration glue, all marked (ours).** About 20 sentences with no source (for example "The cave is cool and dim", "Perhaps he cannot bear for his greatest victory to belong to Nobody.", "The giant, who cannot see, trusts the only sense he has left."). They carry no factual claim but need William's read; they were added to reach 1,550 words without new research.
2. **Homer-details not separately traced in V4**: the stone is rolled back when the giant leaves in the morning (V5-056), the men drive the flock to the ship after the rams (V5-126), and "He saves the best ram for himself, and clings to its belly" (V5-110). All follow Book 9 as I remember it and V4's beats 41-42; flag for the fact-checker.
3. **Hook's "the giant... eats two men at a time"** and "six of them never come out": true across three meals (two each), but "at a time" is a simplification, and it is Odysseus plus six who get out.
4. **Origin paragraph in 97 s**: the Abel hedges and the elephants are tight, but "which is really the nasal cavity" and "dwarf elephants... Ice Age Sicily" compress V4's two sourced beats (C2/N9, C9). "People built those walls, around twelve fifty B.C." rests on Wikipedia (D5), as in V4.
5. **Hook runs 36 s and the closing chapter 97 s**, a little over the 30 s and 60-90 s targets, and the first-eye reveal ("Homer never actually says one eye") now sits at the start of the last chapter, a stronger surprise than before but arriving after the hook has already shown a one-eyed face; shots V5-003, V5-011 and the hook question shot may need a decision on whether to draw one eye or two in the "popular picture" shots.

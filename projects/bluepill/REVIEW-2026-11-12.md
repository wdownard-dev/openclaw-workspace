## Strategist (strategy and risk)
- Biggest risk: slow subscriber conversion. Subscribers gate YPP and views alone don't get there. Unknown Frequencies about 138 lifetime views per subscriber [V]; outlier channels 100-500x [V]; wrdclipz Shorts got 0 subs from 4,642 views [V]. At those rates 1,000 subs needs roughly 140k-500k views [E].
- Second: team capacity. The "1-2 h human time per video" figure is unmeasured. Fact-checking myth variants, shot continuity and thumbnails will exceed it.
- Third: saturation and sameness in "Every ___ Explained". Fourth, AI-content policy: moderate; fully animated non-realistic content needs no label [V]; distinct scripts per video are the defence.
- Sequencing: the pipeline is ahead of the demand evidence (outlier data shows winners only, large survivorship bias). Cheap test: make 3 real 8-10 min videos, publish weekly, write the gate in advance. Gate after 3 videos and 30 days: CTR >= 4%, average view duration >= 35%, and either one video over 10k views or >= 3 subs per 1,000 views [E thresholds].
- YPP timeline [E]: optimistic 3-5 months (about 15%), base 8-14 months (about 50%, 40-60 videos), bad 18+ months or abandon (about 35%). Assumes weekly uploads. Unknown Frequencies took about 9 months to reach 92k [V], a survivor not a median.

Shorts are a capped byproduct (max 2 per video); the 10M Shorts views route is unrealistic and Shorts hours don't count toward 4,000. Missing: thumbnail/title test discipline (3 titles per video, thumbnail A/B), sources line in every description, retention review after each video, a kill criterion, name chosen before first upload, keep it separate from wrdclipz, no banner art spend until video 3.

## Editor (craft; could not judge audio or motion feel, stills only)
- Works: clear six-shot story, character consistency, clean warm palette, accurate word timing.
- Amateur: pacing about 6.5 cuts/min vs about 12 on the reference (shots 8-10 s, last one about 15 s); every shot is the same slow push-in; characters never enter or react; captions cover action and split sentences ("THE GIANT A"); yellow highlight flickers per word; cover-crop at scale 1.17 cuts the Cyclops's head in shot 2; same crossfade everywhere; script has a decent hook but no stakes line or re-hook and ends with no card or tease.
- Top 5 upgrades: (1) 4-5 s per shot, split each narration line into 2-3 images, hard cut on punchlines; (2) cutout entrances with overshoot, bob, simple parallax; (3) smaller captions in dead space, phrase chunks without per-word flicker, one big hand-lettered impact word per beat with pop and shake; (4) information shots: map with animated route arrows, counts, title cards; (5) sound: whooshes, thuds, ducked music bed, chapter stings.
- Volume for 10-12 min: 11-13 shots/min, about 130-150 shots, 80-100 unique images, rest are cutout reuse, crops, insets, reused backgrounds.
- Build first: reusable Scene component (background, timed cutout entrances, camera preset, impact-word slot, sound cues), then three shot types (character scene, map with arrows, title/count card), then a caption component with safe-zone placement.
- Characters and backgrounds: many faces are plain white circles with dot eyes and look too similar; the Cyclops is a big blank circle and the largest thing on screen, give him texture and expression; storyboard backgrounds are flat vector-style while the round-1 backgrounds (storm ship, map) are painterly and stronger, so the style should be settled; cutouts on backgrounds look pasted on, add ground shadows and a colour tint.

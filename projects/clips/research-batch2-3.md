Researched 2026-10-22. Read-only; nobody contacted, nothing joined, no accounts created. Follows the method in `research-notes.md`.

## Method and limits (read first)
- No browser could be launched on this host (no Chrome/Chromium installed). I read Twitch About data (channel bio, panel text/links, social links) through Twitch's public GraphQL endpoint (the same data the About page renders), the YouTube About pages (channel description), and the public X bio meta tags.
- **Gaps:** Panels that are *images only* (no text) came back empty, so any policy written inside a panel image is unseen. Twitch chat commands (`!socials`, `!discord`, `!clips`, etc.) and Discord rules/clip channels were not readable. A "not stated" below means "not found in the text I could read", not "does not exist".
- Discord invite pages were queried only for the public server name and member count; no joining.
- **Nothing here is permission.** All 8 remain `candidate` until a written yes. YouTube's reused-content rules can demonetize a clip-only channel even with the streamer's approval, so plan for real commentary/editing.
- Overall result: **none of the 8 publishes any clip/reupload policy** that I could find.

## Ranking: who to approach, who to skip

Nobody publishes a policy, so the ranking is about **reachability of a decision-maker + advertiser risk**, not about existing consent.

**Approach (in order):**
1. **taxi2g** — direct business Gmail on his own YouTube and X; no agency; lowest sponsor/advertiser risk; large audience (~507K) in a hot game (ARC Raiders); not mature-flagged. Best candidate in this batch.
2. **shinyatheninja** — direct business Gmail across Twitch/YouTube/X; open to growth ("I want to blow up"); has an editor, so frame as a collab. Downside: **mature-flagged channel** (ad/age-restriction risk) and a smaller audience.
3. **nocriesof** — direct personal Gmail in bio; buzzy right now (FACEIT story). Downsides: gambling-adjacent skins sponsor, residual cheating-accusation noise, smaller non-partner channel, possible language barrier.

**Possible but slower (agency/org in the way):**
4. **lyric** — Pivot agency email; clean sponsors; but he is an Embark paid partner so branded content disclosure is a wrinkle.
5. **shotzzy** and 6. **dashy** — OpTic partnerships (plus Underscore for Shotzzy). Large CoD audience, but org/agency sign-off and CDL-rights complications make a quick yes unlikely.

**Skip for now:**
7. **zoomaa** — WME agency + PrizePicks sponsor + CDL/Activision restriction history. Lowest approachability and highest advertiser risk of the eight.
8. **cloakzy** — WME agency address, **Roobet (casino) named in his own X bio**, sub-only Discord. Gambling association is a real monetization risk for a YouTube channel; skip unless William is comfortable with that.

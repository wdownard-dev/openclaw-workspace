# Multiple TikTok accounts: campaign and scheduler side (Morpheus)

Researched 2026-10-26, read-only. Sources: local copies of Vyro Clipper Terms and Content Requirements (campaigns/total-drama-roblox/), MW4 brief, research-buffer.md, buffer.com/pricing fetched 2026-10-26. TikTok-side rules are in tiktok-multi-account-scout.md.

## Vyro (the money side)
- Clipper Terms, Account section: "Limit one (1) Account per person/email address." Also: "You may not create Accounts with multiple email addresses or identities, or use any other device or artifice to create multiple Accounts or participate using multiple identities." Penalty: termination and loss of all Payouts, at Vyro's sole discretion.
  - This is about Vyro accounts, not TikTok accounts. William keeps ONE Vyro account.
  - Terms also say a Clipper registers "at least one" social account and talk about "each Connected Account", so several TikTok accounts owned by William and linked to his one Vyro account look permitted. Not confirmed in writing.
- Rules that still bite with several accounts (campaign rules, TDR and MW4 briefs): own posts only; no duplicate posting (same post cannot be posted twice on one account; our own rule: one submission per post, same clip once per platform per campaign); no botting or engagement farming; no automated posting spam; content must be original and not previously published.
- Using accounts held under other emails/identities that are not William's, or accounts that look like separate people, would look like "multiple identities". That is the one to avoid.
- Per-post payout cap (TDR: $1,000/post, 5,000-view minimum) means extra accounts only add money if each posts DIFFERENT clips. Same clip on N accounts risks duplicate-content flags on TikTok and a duplicate/participating-multiple-times finding at Vyro.

## Buffer
- Free: 3 channels total across all platforms, 10 scheduled posts per channel, 1 API key, 3,000 API requests/month, 250/day. We already use 3 (TikTok rvclips6, Instagram rvclips6, YouTube).
- Essentials $5 per channel per month (billed yearly $60/channel): unlimited scheduled posts, 3 API keys, 7,500 requests/month. Team $10 per channel per month, 5 keys, 15,000 requests.
- Each extra TikTok account = one more paid channel. Example: 3 extra TikTok accounts on Essentials = about $15/month. That is spend; needs William's approval.
- Buffer connects each TikTok channel through TikTok's own login, so it handles several accounts as long as each is a separate channel with its own owner login.

## What this means for "new emails, new hardware"
- New emails: fine for TikTok accounts William genuinely owns. Not fine for creating additional Vyro accounts.
- New hardware: no rule I found in Vyro's terms requires or forbids it. Whether TikTok cares is in Scout's file. Buying hardware to make accounts look unrelated is the ban-evasion line; I'm not researching or recommending that.

# Clip-policy research notes (top 10 GTA V streamers)

Researched 2026-10-21 (read-only; nobody contacted). Sources: streamers' own public X bios, YouTube channel About pages (incl. description text and linked socials), Twitch channel meta/title, Linktree, plus news search.

## Method limits (read first)
- Twitch About pages and panels are JavaScript-rendered. No browser is installed on this host, so I could NOT read any streamer's Twitch panels, chat commands (e.g. Burn's `!shorts`), or Discord rules/channels. Any policy that lives only there is marked **unverified**.
- Discord invites were not joined (no account creation / contact). Invite links below are only those publicly posted on the streamers' own YouTube pages.
- Emails below were read from the streamer's own public X bio or YouTube About page (X obfuscates bio emails; I decoded the standard Cloudflare-protected address).
- Nothing here is permission. All 10 remain `candidate` until a written yes.
- General YouTube caveat (applies to everyone): YouTube's reused-content / monetization rules can demonetize clip-only channels even when the streamer approves. Get permission in writing and plan to add real commentary/editing.

Join the discord and start earning for your clips on all platforms"* with a Discord invite (discord.gg/4C8xWXWwQH). Evolved is a talent agency (esports.gg reports it represents xQc), so this is a **management/agency contact**. X: @xQc.
- **Risks:** Highest competition (his editors and official clip channels post the same content, and a Substack comment alleges his editors have struck clip channels, unverified). Big controversy history (Twitch bans), gambling/Kick-adjacent content on his other streams, frequently reacts to others' videos, so clips can carry third-party copyrighted/Content-ID material. Past YouTube strikes on his own channel (Dexerto). GTA RP clips from his stream will include other players' voices who have not consented. Age-restriction risk is moderate to high.

## Ranked summary: who to approach first

1. **xQc** — the only one with a written, public YouTube-upload rule (24-hour grace period, don't duplicate official channels) and a visible (third-party-reported) paid clipper network. Biggest audience (about 20-25K live). Caveats: his team already saturates the clip space and uploads same-day, so the 24h delay hurts timeliness; go through `xqcyoutube@defiancemgmt.com` / `xqc@evolved.gg`, not DMs. Ask explicitly about Shorts and monetization, since the rule doesn't address them.
2. **PENTA** — has a clean, direct business email (`PentaLiveBiz@gmail.com`) listed on his own X bio and runs his own server (ChaseRP), so one person can plausibly grant permission. No agency in the way. Risk: no stated policy.
3. **Enevun** — direct business email (`Enevun@gmail.com`), cozy/community-friendly brand and the lowest advertiser risk. Mid-size, so a yes is plausible and quick.
4. **pokelawls** — explicit business email on his YouTube (`pokelawlsbusiness@gmail.com`), large audience, and an existing VOD site, though he is a variety streamer and GTA is only part of his content.
5. **novasobored** — direct business email and an editor herself, so she understands clipping; smallest audience, so lower upside.

Hold for now: **Burn** (promising `!shorts` lead, but I couldn't read it and have no contact path; check in a browser first), **Chatterbox** (nothing verified), **erobb221** (agency-style email, rage/profanity risk), **AnthonyZ** and **OfficerMesser** (no public business email; Discord/DM only).

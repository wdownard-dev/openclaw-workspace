Clipper Content Requirements

Clippers must abide by the follow the following Clipper Content Requirements (“Requirements”) when creating and posting Clipper Content or resharing Client Content in connection with the Client Campaigns and the Vyro Platform.

1. Disclose Your Connections 

In accordance with the FTC Guides, you must make your connection to the Clients clear to readers, if you are discussing your participation in a Client Campaign or use of the Platform. You must make your connection to Client(s) clear to readers and third parties, if you are discussing or engaging with a Client Campaign that relates to a particular Client.

2. Give Your Honest and Truthful Opinions

Only make claims about a product or service if provided to you in the Client Campaign details or requirements. Do not make claims about Client competitors without Client’s approval or Vyro competitors without Vyro’s approval. Your statements should always reflect your honest and truthful opinions and actual experiences. If a statement is not your opinion, but rather something we or a Client asked you to say, this fact should be made clear to readers.

3. Only Make Factual Statements That Are Truthful and Can Be Verified

Only make a factual statement about a Client/Client Campaign/product/service’s characteristic or quality which is truthful, accurate, and non-misleading. Remember that even if you don't expressly state a fact, it may be implied, and the FTC Guides apply to both express and implied messages.

Only discuss the Platform in a truthful and accurate manner consistent with these Requirements, the Terms, and other materials or instructions provided by Vyro to you from time to time. Do not make claims or representations of potential or guaranteed income or profits in connection with the Platform, or make projections, claims or estimates regarding other participants’ potential, hypothetical, or guaranteed income from the Platform. Do not disclose your own income from the Platform.

4. Respect Intellectual Property Rights

“Intellectual property” is the group of legal rights to things people create or invent. Intellectual property rights typically include copyright, trademark, and trade secret rights, as well as the right to use someone's name, likeness or voice. Only use Client intellectual property as set forth in the Client Campaign details, with express permission, and do not alter or modify any Client intellectual property or Client Content. Only use third-party owned intellectual property with express written permission from the third party.

5. Get Permission for Promotions

You are not authorized to conduct a promotion (sweepstakes, contest, free giveaway, etc.) for Client, using Client products, or for Vyro. Please contact Vyro before proceeding.

6. You Are Personally Responsible For Your Actions

You are liable under federal and state law for your actions with respect to your participation in the Client Campaigns, which makes it critical that you follow these Requirements, as well as all other applicable laws and regulations, including but not limited to the FTC Guides and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act. You should know that we will hold you directly responsible for any claims which arise from your violation of these Terms, or our rights.

7. No Botting, Stealing, or Reposting

Using bots or other similar means to inflate views, impressions, metrics, and/or your performance is prohibited.

All Clipper Content must be your own and posting another Clipper’s Clipper Content is prohibited.

Posting the same Clipper Content multiple times on the same Clipper Account is prohibited.

8. Clipper Content Policy

Clipper Content that violates any of the following standards may be removed or rejected, and may subject a Clipper to a permanent ban from the Platform and other penalties:

Illegal Activity: Clipper Content that violates any laws or promotes illegal conduct.

Drugs and Alcohol: Clipper Content that promotes, encourages or facilitates the sale of alcohol, drugs (including substances that mimic them), or drug-related paraphernalia.

Violence and Harm: Clipper Content that promotes, encourages, threatens, or incites violence, abuse, self-harm, or the harm of others.

Hate Speech and Discrimination: Clipper Content that promotes or encourages, or perpetuates racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or other forms of discrimination or hate.

Harassment and Bullying: Clipper Content that targets directly or indirectly individuals with repeated or severe abuse, threats, or harassment.

Sexual and Adult Content: Pornography or similar video/static content, sexually explicit material, or sexually suggestive content involving minors (real or implied).

Deceptive or Misleading Claims: Including fake earnings/revenue statements, deceptive health claims, or impersonation or deepfakes of individuals or organizations.

Misinformation: Clipper Content that spreads demonstrably false information, particularly relating to elections or public health and safety.

Sensitive or Graphic Content: Excessively violent, gory, or distressing material.

Infringement: Clipper Content that leaks or distributes proprietary content or uses copyrighted or trademarked material without proper rights or attribution.

Privacy Violations: Clipper Content that includes collecting or sharing private or confidential information (e.g., home addresses, phone numbers, financial information) without consent.

Impersonation and Fraud: Pretending to be someone else, misrepresenting your identity or affiliation, or accessing someone else's Account without authorization.

Unauthorized Data Access: Using bots, scrapers, or automation to collect or manipulate platform data.

Unregistered Financial Services: Clipper Content that includes promotion of services such as loan repayment, debt collection, or personalized investment advice without proper authorization/substantiation.

9. Artificial Engagement

Any attempt to manipulate views, impressions, or other performance metrics through artificial methods is strictly prohibited. This includes but is not limited to:

Using bots, view farms, click generators, or fake accounts.

Coordinated attempts to inflate views, impressions, likes, shares, or other forms of engagement.

Misleading/deceptive metadata or tags to boost visibility.

10. Advertising and Self-Promotion

Clippers may promote their own content and campaigns, but must not:

Promote Client or its campaigns through spam (e.g., mass messaging, comment spam, or automated posting).

Run paid advertisements (e.g., PPC, boosted posts) using the Client name or branding without prior written permission.

11. We Reserve The Right To Require You To Remove Content

By participating in the Client Campaigns or using the Platforms, you agree that you will immediately remove, take down, or delete any published content relating to Vyro, Client(s), either of our products or services, or those of either of our competitors, from any site or platform immediately upon our request. Even if we do not notify you, we expect that you will promptly notify us of any complaint or content of which you later become aware may be in violation of the law or violate third party rights.

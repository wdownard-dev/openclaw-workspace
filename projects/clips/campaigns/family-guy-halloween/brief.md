Details

This campaign promotes the Family Guy Halloween Special (2026), now streaming on Hulu and Hulu on Disney+, through short-form clips posted across social platforms. The goal is to drive large-scale reach and cultural relevance around this year's special, while encouraging viewers to watch the full episode on Hulu and Hulu on Disney+.

*Collab posts and Story posts are NOT allowed.*

*Do not post the same video on multiple accounts. Each submission should be unique.*

What to Clip

You have a lot of creative freedom with how you post the 2026 Halloween Special. Get creative with your posts. Some formats are listed below:

Make memes or add POV text
Create edits
Top 5 moments compilation with the funniest or best moments from the episode
Top 5 moments compilation with the funniest or best moments for a specific character
Post clips as-is

Rules

Mandatory Caption Requirements

Allowed Content

Clips must come exclusively from the provided content folders
No unrelated footage or outside visuals

Do not create new plots, storylines, or characters, or change the core of the original content

You may add POV or meme text. Any added text must be appropriate for, and not negative toward, Disney, Hulu, and Family Guy.

If you use any AI tools while editing, only use tools that do not train on, or otherwise reuse, the material you upload.

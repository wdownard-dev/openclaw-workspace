## 3. What the winners have in common

1. **Commentary on top of official or viral footage.** Ugo Lord runs 15 Shorts, all reactions, and gained +70K in Oct 2026. The Civil Rights Lawyer adds voice commentary and follow-up posts. Both fit the reused-content "allowed" examples (S1). Caveat: neither is a news channel.
2. **A repeated title frame.** Police Bodycam USA uses series titles ("Wannabe Tough Guy…") across all 15 Shorts. Law By Mike uses "Who Can Escape The X Fastest?". The Civil Rights Lawyer uses "UPDATE" / "FINAL UPDATE".
3. **Question-form hooks.** Police Bodycam Arrests HQ uses questions that name a legal issue, and reached 455.9K average views per video.
4. **Shorts-led channels with a long-form pointer.** The Civil Rights Lawyer says "full video at link in bio" in Reels. Caught! In Action is long-form heavy and grew only +1K in 30 days, so long-form alone did not drive growth.
5. **Posting cadence is high where data is given.** Arrests HQ: 5.6/week. Clip Sheriff: 500 videos in ~23 months, about 5/week (my calculation).
6. **Subscriber gains come in spikes.** Law By Mike gained 99,974 subs on one day (Nov 4, 2026), tied to one Short. Police Bodycam Videos gained 0 subs in 28 days.

No fetched source documents a 0-to-1,000 path with dates. The closest is Police Bodycam Arrests HQ (about 1.4 years to 115K, per CreatorRecon).

## 5. YouTube policy (reused and inauthentic content)

- **Reused content (S1).** Allowed: clips for critical review; reaction videos that comment on the original; reuse where the creator is visible or explains how they added to it. Not allowed: clips "with little or no narrative"; "Short videos you compiled from other social media websites"; content "downloaded or copied… without any substantive modifications"; text readings from news feeds with nothing else added. The policy "applies even if you have permission from the original creator." Monetization can be removed from the whole channel.
- **Inauthentic content** (renamed from "repetitious" on July 15, 2025; S1). Content that is mass-produced or repetitive is not monetizable.
- **Shorts (S2).** Unedited clips from others' shows, reuploads of other creators' content, and compilations with no original content are ineligible for engaged views. From Feb 1, 2027, Shorts revenue sharing requires 10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days.
- **General guidance (S3, Apr 8, 2025).** Consistency, a strong first few seconds, trending audio, linking to other content. No subscriber rates.

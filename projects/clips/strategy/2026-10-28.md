## 3. What is not working, and what to stop
- **STOP defaulting every live post into a Vyro slot.** All 6 slots went to the first posts live (views 0, 128, 0, 2, 0, 302), not to the best ones. mw4-08 (8.5k) was never submitted because MW4 was already blocked. Evidence: vyro-submissions.json, buffer-snapshots.csv. Caveat: the "approval only at 5k" rule is William's report, not verified; 0 approvals in 5 to 6 days is consistent with it.
- **STOP separate Instagram variants for HardScope** until IG is linked in Content Rewards. Unlinked IG posts cannot be submitted, and the 30-min window means they can never count later. IG evidence: n=8 posts, max 167 views.
- **STOP burst posting.** Tonight is 4 to 5 TikToks in 4 hours on a 5-day-old account. Our own research note says 1 to 3 per day per platform for new accounts. It also makes per-clip readings non-independent.
- **STOP expanding the search-phrase test.** "Modern Warfare 4:" caption group (mw4-12, 13, 16) median 186 vs plain group (mw4-01, 07, 08) median 756. Clips, slots and ages differ, so no conclusion, but no support either. MW4 can't take a clean 5-clip batch now.
- **STOP reading Buffer views within 24 h.** Buffer showed 0 views for mw4-08 11 hours after post while TikTok showed 8,041 at 2.75 h. results.csv `views_48h` is blank on every row; the only usable readings live in notes. Ask for fixed-age readings (3 h, 24 h, 72 h) from the TikTok app.
- **Unresolved, not a content signal:** the 6 YouTube Shorts with zero distribution (never investigated), and 2 of 2 TDR posts below 400 views (n=2, not enough data).
- **Open risk:** Vyro's terms ban "automated posting" and our submissions are browser-automated; the support question (outbox/vyro-support-draft.md) was never sent. Whop's rules on automation are unread. Worth asking before any money is at stake.

# Clip-policy research, batch 2 / part 4 (formal, doublelift, dantes, sheiden, humzh, imperialhal__, mgclutch, hivise)

Researched 2026-10-22. Read-only; nobody contacted, no accounts created, no Discords joined.

## Method and limits
- **Twitch About/panels:** No browser is installed on this host (the browser tool failed: "No supported browser found"). Instead I read each channel's public About text, social links and panels (text, link targets) from Twitch's public web endpoint (`gql.twitch.tv`, the same data a logged-out visitor's page loads). Image-only panels (no text, no link) could not be read; none of the 8 had text-less panels that looked like rules, but a rule printed inside a panel image is **unverified**.
- **X/Twitter:** bios read from the public profile pages. The handle in the candidate list is not always the streamer's X handle; correct handles are given below. Emails shown are decoded from X's standard email-obfuscation.
- **YouTube About:** read from each channel's About page (description + linked socials). Business emails on YouTube are hidden behind a "view email" captcha, so a YouTube-only email is **unverified** unless it also appears elsewhere.
- **Not readable:** Discord rules/channels (not joined), chat commands (`!clips`, `!shorts`, etc.), Instagram/TikTok bios, Linktree-type pages not linked from the above. Any clip policy living only there is **unverified**.
- **No explicit clip/reupload policy was found for any of the 8.** All eight are `not stated (unverified)`. Nothing here is permission.
- **Platform-level caveat (applies to all):** Third-party reporting from Oct 2026 says YouTube is reducing Shorts distribution for re-uploads that add little original work, and the existing reused-content rule can demonetize clip-only channels even with the streamer's permission. Sources: https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/10/youtube-cuts-reach-reuploaded-shorts-originality-push.html and https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/youtube-cracks-down-on-clipping-channels-in-shorts-update-prioritizing-original-content-3415700/ (I could only read search summaries of these; the YouTube primary post was not checked). Plan for real commentary/editing.

## Ranked: who to approach, and who to skip

Everyone starts at `candidate`. None has a stated policy, so all outreach is a cold ask for written permission (Shorts, monetized, credit + link back).

1. **Sheiden** - best fit. Direct personal business email on his own Twitch panel (`sheiden.contact@gmail.com`), no agency in the way, gameplay-only content, modest risk. Confirm any team-contract limits when asking.
2. **FORMAL** - `partnerships@opticgaming.com` is an official partnerships inbox on his Twitch About, and CoD content is relatively clean. Downsides: an org will want terms, he already posts his own highlights/reactions, and PrizePicks/Monster sponsors.
3. **humzh** - email listed on both Twitch and X (Accel Media, agency-style). Big audience, but a 28-day subathon creates lots of low-value VOD; ask which segments are fair game.
4. **ImperialHal__** - clear email (`imperialhal@liftsm.com`), but he is a contracted Falcons/Red Bull pro posting his own highlights, and tournament footage is likely organizer IP. Worth asking only for his solo-stream (non-tournament) content.
5. **mgclutch** - reachable only by DM/Discord (no email) and he already publishes his own Shorts. Approach only if you want a low-cost, low-upside conversation.
6. **Doublelift** - agency contact only (Mythic Talent) and profanity-heavy persona; a larger audience but a poor monetization fit. Lower priority.

**Skip for now:**
- **Dantes** - WME agency contact, an openly "toxic" brand in his own FAQ, past tournament controversy, and heavy profanity; poor risk/reward for a monetized channel.
- **hivise** - the content in question is mostly Jynxzi's, which he cannot license; only revisit if you target Jynxzi directly (separate research).

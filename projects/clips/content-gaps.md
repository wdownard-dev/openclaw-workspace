## Rules for using a phrase
1. Only where it honestly describes the clip. A search phrase never overrides truth, the hook-matches-first-3-seconds rule, or the campaign brief.
2. MW4 brief still applies: only #MW4 and #MW4partner as hashtags; captions and hooks must differ clip to clip (same phrase, different sentence); no engagement bait.
3. Put the phrase as plain words in: spoken/hook line where the footage truly says it, the on-screen text, and the start of the post caption. Do not stuff several phrases into one post.
4. Several posts per topic is the point: plan batches of 5+ around one phrase when the footage allows. Vary the rest.
5. Log the phrase in the spec (`search_phrase`) and in playbook.md so views can be compared. One variable at a time where possible.

## Scout
When ranking candidate moments for MW4 or any new campaign, add a "search fit" note: which phrase from the list above the moment can honestly carry (or "none"), and flag moments that suit a 5-clip batch on one phrase. Do not rank a clip higher than its footage deserves just to match a phrase.

## Editor
Write the phrase into hook/on-screen text/captions where truthful, add `search_phrase` to the spec, and keep batches testable (control clip without a phrase when you can).

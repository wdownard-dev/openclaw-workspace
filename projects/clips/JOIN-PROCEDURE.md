# Joining a Vyro campaign on William's behalf (his rule, 2026-10-23)
William reads the terms and replies "Accept" on Telegram or webchat, then answers a one-line "Confirm" check (changed 2026-10-24: he no longer types the campaign name); Morpheus then clicks Join. Scout never clicks anything; this procedure is Morpheus's.

## 1. Package (Scout reads, Morpheus assembles; read-only until step 3)
Send William ONE message per campaign containing:
- Campaign name, client, platforms, rate per 1,000 views, min views per post, days left, % paid out, footage link type.
- The campaign's full rules/terms text exactly as shown in the Join dialog (not paraphrased), plus the links to the general documents it binds him to (Content Requirements https://vyro.com/content-requirements, Clipper Terms https://vyro.com/clipper-terms). Summarize the general documents but flag every clause on: automation/scheduling/bots/scrapers, payouts and fees, exclusivity, minimum time commitments, takedown/clawback, IP and licence grants, liability, dispute/arbitration, account termination, and anything that differs from the last campaign joined.
- Say plainly what I could not read and that "Accept" means he agreed to the terms himself.
- Never include or ask for payment, identity or tax details.
- End the message with: "Reply Accept to join this campaign, or tell me what to change." Do not ask him to type the campaign name.

## 2. Consent (two steps, both required)
Step A, "Accept": a message from William that is just Accept (any case; "Accept" plus the name is also fine). It counts only if ALL hold: (1) exactly one join package is pending, i.e. I send one package at a time and send no second package until the first is accepted, declined or withdrawn; (2) it arrived after the package and, where the channel supports it, replies to or directly follows that package; (3) the terms have not changed since he read them. Otherwise it is not consent: ask again.
Step B, "Confirm": after Accept, I reply with one line that restates the campaign name, creator/client and rate (e.g. "Join Total Drama Roblox (Sinjin), $X/1k views? Reply Confirm"). Only an explicit Confirm after that line triggers the click. This is where the campaign is named, so he does not have to type it, and it catches a stale or misdirected Accept (the brand name is shared by several campaigns).
Not consent: silence, "ok", "sure", "go", "yes" or "y" at step A, an Accept sent before he could have seen the package, an Accept left over from an older package, or Confirm without a preceding Accept for that exact package. One consent covers one campaign and one Join click, nothing else. Re-send the package (and restart at step A) if the terms changed since he read it.

## 3. Click
Open the campaign's Join dialog in one pinned tab, confirm the title and terms text still match the package (compare a few distinctive sentences), click the single Join/Accept button, then re-read My campaigns to confirm it shows as joined.
Stop and tell William, without clicking further, if: the dialog asks for payment, identity, tax/payout setup, a phone or email code, a new document not in the package, a checkbox the package did not mention, or anything unexpected.

## 4. Record
Log in PLAN.md: campaign, time, the message ids of his Accept and Confirm, outcome. Then the normal flow: footage fetch, Editor, review gate, schedule.

## Limits
- Vyro's rules include a ban on "automated posting" and on using "bots, scrapers, or automation" to collect or manipulate platform data. Clicking Join through the browser tool may fall under that wording; unresolved (see outbox/vyro-support-draft.md). William knows and owns that risk.
- Payout setup, cash-out, connecting accounts and any form with personal or financial details stay manual for William.

How The Wanted System Works In GTA 6

The stars in GTA 6 aren't just a number anymore. Here's exactly what each one tells you.

- Hollow stars: they know a crime happened but not that it was you. Walk away quietly.
- Solid white stars: they can see you and they're chasing you.
- Red stars: you got away, but you're still in the heat area. Come back too soon and the stars return.
- Red icons under your stars show what the cops know: your look, your outfit, your car, your gun, a camera catching you, and your partner.
- Knock them out one by one: change your hair, throw on a spare outfit from the trunk, swap cars, or hit a Pay 'n' Spray and ditch the gun.
- Cops looking for a couple? Split up. One keeps them busy, the other runs.
- Expensive sports cars can have trackers.
- Cops are not on your mini-map anymore. One star is easy to lose; all six red stars means a full-map search.
